## Would I, from my seat

- **Would I do the first step?** Yes. Install, grant full Photos access, tap Index, leave it on the charger overnight. That is the easiest ask any photo tool has made of me. But it only indexes the photos *I* shot.
- **What do I have to give up?** For Solo, nothing. For Crew, my techs give up privacy: the Crew plan pushes 512 px thumbnails and embeddings of their camera rolls to a company index, with "not a job" filtering that rests on a clustering precision the variant marks [unknown]. My 22-year-old apprentice will not agree to that, independent of "another app".
- **Who do I trust?** On-device indexing with originals staying in iCloud is the right answer for Solo. The Crew plan asks me to trust a startup's server with my crew's phones.
- **What if you die?** Better than most: originals never left iCloud, the index is rebuildable, packets are PDFs I can keep. The 15-year packet archive is a promise I should assume dies with you, so I would keep every PDF myself, which also means it is not your switching cost.
- **Cost vs today?** Today: $0 plus the dispute I lost. $144/yr is cheap against Jobber ($348–588/yr) and CompanyCam Core ($756/yr annual). Cheap is not the same as worth it if two-thirds of my photos are not in it.
- **Would I recommend it?** Only after it wins me a dispute. The variant's own assumption 3 says dispute frequency per contractor per year is [unknown], so the referral loop has an unknown clock.

## Most damaging objection

**"Of my 30 photos a day, 20–25 are shot by my techs and arrive in my iMessage threads. Your app reads my camera roll through PhotoKit. Those photos are not in my camera roll. My crew will not install your app. So on day one you have indexed my 5–10 photos a day, and the evidence packet for my next dispute is missing the tech's photo of the pre-existing damage. What am I paying $144 a year for?"**

Why it ends the conversation: the variant's one-line pitch is "indexes the job photos a small contractor already has in the camera roll (and the ones crew texted them)". The second clause has no support anywhere in research/: PhotoKit enumerates the Photos library (platform-access.md Q1), and nothing in the 19 files addresses Messages attachments, the "Shared with You" surface, or whether a photo saved from Messages keeps its EXIF capture date and GPS. The variant itself marks iMessage GPS [unknown] and says that is "why crew-phone pooling, not message scraping, is the design" — which hands the problem to the Day-7 crew install that this customer has already refused. For a one-person shop the objection vanishes; for the 1–9 employee shop the variant names as its customer, it is the whole job.
