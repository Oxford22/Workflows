## Bottom line
1. **No — the evidence does not support a nine-figure outcome in this category.** The largest *sourced* revenue is Remento: "exceeded $7 million in sales" in "less than a year since we closed our deal", a founder statement in a Shark Tank update segment that aired 2026-03-25 [sourced-secondary: S19, 2026-06-05; S20, 2026-08-09; air date S71, S72]. It is unaudited, "sales" is undefined (gross or net), the deal-closing date is not given, and the statement is at least 8 months old [estimated: 2026-11-25 − 2026-03-25]. Storyworth, the leader, has never disclosed revenue: **[unknown]**. No estimate of it is solid enough for this line; two labelled illustrations are in Q1.
2. $100M a year at a ~$90 net price needs ~1.1M paid plans a year [estimated: 100,000,000 ÷ 90]. That is about the same number as the *floor* Storyworth advertises for books printed since 2013, "Over 1,000,000" [sourced: S1]; the true book count is unknown, so this is a comparison against a floor [estimated: 1.1M vs ≥ 1.0M].
3. Both wedges of the round-3 variant already ship at incumbents: an automated phone interviewer with follow-up questions (Storyworth "Magic Interviews", works on a landline [sourced: S1, S3, S5]; Tell Mel from $39 [sourced: S29]; Memorygram, per a competitor [sourced-secondary: S69]) and photos used as prompts (Remento [sourced: S11, S12]; Storyworth [sourced: S1]). On 2026-10-22 Remento launched a photo-anchored product, an ornament with "one photo, every story behind it" at $29.99 [sourced: S76].
4. The residual gap is narrower than first written. One small app, Memory Murals, already sells a photo-first family archive where "People & dates [are] tagged automatically from your voice" for $99.99 a year, with 100+ Android downloads [sourced: S92]. A 2024 academic prototype (GoodTimes) did AI voice conversations over a user's photos backed by a knowledge graph of people, places and time, with 13 participants [sourced: S93]. What nobody found does is the combination: an AI interviewer that calls the elder, works through the family's photos, and returns a named, dated archive plus a book.
5. AI voice cost is not the constraint: ~$6–$22 per recipient-year for the mid-priced stacks, $6–$51 across every listed price, ~$9–$67 with a 30% overhead for retries plus chapter writing [estimated: §2 Q5]. The constraints are completion (≈ 10 stories per Remento family against 52 prompts [estimated: Q2]), a one-shot purchase with optional renewal, Q4 seasonality, undisclosed CAC, and a failure list that grew in this repair: Artifact (shut), HereAfter AI (site 404), StoryFile (Chapter 11), StoryTerrace (administration, then liquidation), LifeBook (two insolvencies), No Story Lost (closed), Eternos (pivot), Autobiographer (App Store listing gone).

6. The gift market for aging parents: NRF spend, price points, gift share of Storyworth purchases.
7. CAC and retention for gift subscriptions: renewal, CAC, podcast CPMs, affiliate rates.
8. Falsification: saturation, stalling, non-completion.
