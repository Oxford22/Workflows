# Critique: v1-estate — Round 1 — Target-customer lens

Written 2026-11-03. Fresh-context critic. Read in order: SCORECARD.md, variants/v1-estate.md, every file in research/ (including research/assumptions/). Did not read CHANGELOG, other variants, or other critiques.

## Stance

I am an executor in my 50s handling my father's estate. I have his iPhone and his passcode; he never set a Legacy Contact. The memorial is in nine days and the funeral home wants photos. I am not technical. I am grieving. I am already writing cheques to the funeral home and to a probate attorney. The questions I score against: would I actually do the first step, what do I have to give up, who do I trust, what happens if the startup dies, what does this cost me versus what I do today, and would I recommend it to a peer. Pain, Wedge and Distribution are scored hardest, as instructed.

The persona matters for this variant: the variant's "no-access majority" is Legacy-Contact-less heirs; its "phone in hand, passcode known" path is mine. That path has the fewest clocks and the most free alternatives, which is why several scores below come in under the variant's own self-assessment.

## Most damaging objection

**"My funeral director already texted me a link where the whole family uploads Dad's photos, the slideshow is in the package I'm paying for, and I'm holding Dad's unlocked iPhone with nothing deleting what's on it. What does your free step do on day 2 that the link doesn't, and why would I pay you $149 on day 14 when the service is over and the only deadline I had is gone?"**

The research facts behind it: Tukios serves 10,000+ homes and gives each one "a shareable link to send via email or text, allowing families to upload their photos and videos directly" [research/estate.md Q6]; funeral homes need "Typically 30 to 100 photos" "A day or two before the service" and the slideshow is "often included in the package at no extra charge" [research/estate.md Q8]; for a passcode holder the only clocks are Legacy-Contact (never started) and Google's two-year inactivity, so "the variant's premise ... describes the second [moment], which has no forcing function except the deletion clocks" [research/estate.md Q8 interpretation]; and in 156 posts and 264 comments across r/widowers, r/Genealogy, r/GooglePhotos and others, "nobody tells a poster to pay a person or service to merge, transfer, recover or organise a digital library" [research/trades-and-reddit.md]. If the founder cannot say what the tribute pack does that the Tukios link does not, and cannot name the clock that makes me buy at week two, the conversation ends.

## Weakest dimension

**Distribution (3).** Three dimensions tie at 3 (Compounding asset, Buildability, Ceiling), but Distribution is the one where the research is actively adverse rather than merely absent: the only touchpoint that reaches me is the funeral home, and research/estate.md Q6 concludes the funeral-home photo moment is already owned by Tukios/Tribute and that aftercare programmes are "cards and texts, not data services". The variant's own paid-channel arithmetic ($300 CAC vs $126 contribution) fails unless the un-researched Tukios $99/mo comparable holds. Every other weakness could be survived if the funeral director handed me this product; without him, I never hear of it.

## Unknowns to research before the next round

1. Of a sample of 50 US funeral-home General Price Lists (FTC Funeral Rule requires one be given to families), what share include a tribute video/slideshow in the package at no charge, what share list it as a paid add-on, and at what price? (Resolves whether the day-2 job is already free for most families.)
2. What does the privacy.apple.com "Get a copy of your data" iCloud Photos bundle actually contain for a 10,000-item library — albums, People names, edited and original versions, Live Photo pairs — how large is it, and how many days does Apple take to prepare it? (A cooperating living account can answer this; the variant's week-4 run should not be the first time anyone looks.)
3. What are Tukios' current aftercare products and prices (Aftercare-By-Text, cards), and does Tukios or Tribute Technology offer any family-facing photo collection or organisation beyond the tribute-video upload link? (Verifies the variant's un-researched $99/mo comparable and the "slot is unfilled" claim.)
4. What share of adult children of a recently deceased parent knew the parent's phone passcode, and what share of decedents had an Apple Legacy Contact set? (A nat-rep or bereavement-panel survey question; the variant's 20–50% working band is an assumption.)
5. What happens to a deceased person's iCloud Photos library when the iCloud+ subscription lapses and the account exceeds the free 5 GB — is anything deleted, and on what timeline? (Determines whether a passcode-holding heir faces any clock at all.)

## Three questions for the founder

1. Walk me through one real executor who had the unlocked phone: what did they do for the funeral slideshow, what did the funeral home give them, and what did it cost them? Which of those steps does your product remove?
2. Have you, personally, downloaded a privacy.apple.com photo export for an account with more than 5,000 photos? Did it contain albums, People names and capture dates, and how long did Apple take to prepare it?
3. My funeral director already sends me the Tukios link. What exact sentence does he say in the arrangement conference to get me to open your page instead, and what is in it for him beyond the $99/month you hope he will pay you?
