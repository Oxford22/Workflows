## Most damaging objection

**Of the ~460 active Photo Managers, how many bill hourly — and why would an hourly organizer pay $1,260 a year to delete the hours on her own invoice?** The variant's seat price is justified as "replacing billable hours", but the research has no answer to who loses those hours: 2 of the 6 priced operator pages are hourly, the membership split is [unknown] (pro-channel.md Q7), and no organizer statement about AI and billable hours was found. The only software vendor that spent three years in this exact channel (Mylio, 2023–2025 sponsor, Concierge delivered by certified members) is gone from the 2026 sponsor list, which the research calls the only evidence that "the channel did not pay". If the founder cannot show either a membership billing-model split or a fixed-fee pricing playbook that makes the saved hours mine rather than the client's, the buyer the pricing assumes may be a minority of the 700 — and I am not in it.

## Weakest dimension

**Ceiling (3)**, tied with Compounding asset (3). The variant's own arithmetic caps the seat business under $10M ARR at total penetration of five professional bodies, and the exported-by-design deliverable — the thing that makes me willing to try it — is what prevents anything from compounding. Of the three dimensions scored hardest, Distribution (4) is the weakest: the "non-paid" channel is a paid sponsorship package with one negative precedent.

## Unknowns to research before the next round

1. What share of The Photo Managers members price digital consolidation hourly versus fixed-fee / per-source / per-phase? (Census of ≥50 certified-organizer pricing pages, or the TPM member survey question on billing model.)
2. What share of professional photo organizers work on macOS versus Windows? (TPM member demographic survey; FOREVER Historian and Vivid-Pix Memory Station being Windows-only; Mylio platform-split statements.)
3. Why did Mylio, FOREVER, Vivid-Pix and Photomyne drop off the 2026 TPM sponsor list after 2025, what do TPM year-round partner packages cost, and did Mylio Concierge sell? (Vendor interviews; prospectus archive; Mylio support/IR statements.)
4. On a real 3-source family project (Google Takeout + Apple Photos library + external drive), what is the organizer's measured touch-time by step, and what fraction is sidecar repair, dedupe and naming? (Time-and-motion with 3–5 certified organizers.)
5. Does Mylio (and Lightroom Classic) import XMP `PersonInImage` + MWG regions with `AppliedToDimensions` as face tags on re-import, so the workbench's deliverable lands in the catalog an organizer already uses? (Mylio manual / support; KDE bug 429219 pattern for Lightroom.)

## Three questions for the founder

1. On your first real client library, what was the measured touch-time before and after, and what was the ledger's item-accounting rate? If it is below 99.5%, what do I say to the family about the missing items — and who is liable, you or me?
2. I bill $85/hr. What do you want me to invoice the client for the hours your tool removed? Show me one hourly organizer who adopted a tool like this and earned more the following year, or give me the fixed-fee playbook you expect me to switch to.
3. If you shut down in 18 months, which of these keep working on my Mac without your server: the Takeout matcher, the Photos.sqlite reader (who patches it next September?), my provenance ledgers and consent records, and the families' vaults — and in what format do I get them out?
