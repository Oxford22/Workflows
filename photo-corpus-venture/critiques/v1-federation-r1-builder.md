## Numbers the variant asserts that the research contradicts or does not support

1. **"~15 min for 30,000 photos, ~22 min derated"** (Wedge step 2) and **acceptance "≤30 min"** (Buildability). research/ios-feasibility.md Q3 is explicit: "The 15-minute figure is a floor built from batch-1 model benchmarks, not a central estimate." Central is ~38 min, derated ~57 min, and the ×1.5 derate was applied to the floor, not the central. The variant understates by 2.5–4×. **Failed claim.**
2. **"A 30k-photo library is therefore searchable within the first session."** Depends on (a) the share of assets with no local copy, which ios-feasibility.md Q4 marks [unknown] ("Apple evicts by storage pressure; no rule published"), and (b) the task surviving: Apple DTS says expiry is final and opening the Camera kills a `BGContinuedProcessingTask` ("There isn't anything you can do to prevent it"). **Unsupported.**
3. **Week 2: "`BGContinuedProcessingTask` wrapper ... chunked and resumable."** ios-feasibility.md Q2 quotes DTS: "No. The idea is that the API is specifically extending foreground time, so it wouldn't really make sense to 'resume' it later"; queued resubmission is "probably not reliable enough to be useful". The app can only ask the user to tap again. **Contradicted.**
4. **Week 2: ANE work in the background.** iOS 27 "now restricts background access to the Neural Engine"; the `continued-processing.inference` entitlement has no documented request process and one developer report says it is absent from the 27.2 SDK (ios-feasibility.md Q2). Without it, a backgrounded index runs on CPU (GPU needs its own entitlement). **Platform-gated.**
5. **Week 3 vs Week 4.** Week 3: per-member bundle "encrypted with a family key the company does not hold". Week 4: "a remote MCP server over the family index with OAuth and a Claude install link". research/byo-ai.md Q2: Claude's connector reaches a server on the "Public internet, from Anthropic IP ranges". A server that can answer Claude's search holds plaintext vectors and previews; a server that holds only ciphertext cannot search. The variant's economics assume a server-side vector DB ($0.05/member) *and* an index the company cannot read. **Internal contradiction** — and it is the hinge for the BIPA defence (face-regulation.md Q1: Samsung turned on the company never having access; Apple lost its encryption argument at summary judgment).
6. **Why-now #3 / Week 1: "MobileCLIP2 ... MIT code".** research/ai-why-now.md Q1: code MIT, weights "Apple ML Research Model TOU ('apple-amlr')". Whether the weights may ship in a commercial App Store binary is not established anywhere in research/. **Licence gap, not addressed.**
7. **"face embedding (<4 ms/face per Apple 2021)".** That is Apple's internal model with no public API; the variant's actual plan is an AdaFace Core ML port whose iPhone latency is [unknown] (ios-feasibility.md Q3), and iOS 27 serialises concurrent Core ML predictions, so it cannot be hidden behind the CLIP pass. The variant flags this itself; the throughput numbers in the Wedge section still use the 4 ms figure.
8. **Distribution #3: "4 of 7 pricing pages found are fixed-fee"** — research/pro-channel.md Q7 says 4 of 6 *priced* pages (7 cited, one never rendered). Minor.

Everything else I checked (iCloud+/Google One prices, FamilyAlbum 30M, Apple Ads CPI, RevenueCat renewal rates, Mylio Family pricing, Immich 3.3.0, TelemetryDeck device shares, PCC <2M downloads, App Attest billing, G.T. v. Samsung, Shared Library caps) matches the research files.

## What breaks at 10k families

- **Cost:** compute does not break (on-device). The unmodeled lines are support, Claude/Gemini via App Attest (org-level cap; "metered add-on priced later"), and a remote MCP host if it exists. Apple's standard commission applies above $1M proceeds.
- **Support:** "Person 7" never resolves for the aunt who will not install; face-regulation.md Q7's worked scenario leaves 19–46% of appearances un-named. Every un-named relative is a "can't find Grandma" ticket, and assumption 9's falsifier is >20% of tickets about "can't find". Consent screens for non-members are a second ticket class.
- **Data-loss liability:** a company-unreadable family key with no stated recovery path means a lost phone can delete the only thing that compounds (confirmed identities, annotations). This is also the trust story the pitch depends on.
- **Legal exposure:** on-device templates are the Samsung defence; the moment face vectors sync (ciphertext or not) the Apple iCloud-subclass theory applies, and which Apple subclass survived on 2026-09-30 is [unknown] (face-regulation.md Q1). IL+TX are 12.7% of the US population; BIPA damages are $1,000–$5,000 per person. The variant geofences only "server-side face matching", which it does not do anyway — so nothing is actually geofenced.

## Most damaging objection

**Run it. Take a real 30,000-asset iCloud-optimised library on an iPhone 15 (not Pro) and an iPhone 16, tap Index, and report: wall-clock, share of assets skipped as `PHImageResultIsInCloudKey`, and what happened when the user opened Camera at minute 20.** The variant's spec promises ≤30 minutes in one session; the only evidence in research/ puts the central estimate at 38–57 minutes, the no-local-copy share is unknown, Apple DTS says the task cannot resume after expiry and is killed by the Camera, and running the Neural Engine in the background on iOS 27 requires an entitlement whose request process is undocumented. If a founder cannot produce that measurement — which a Queryable fork can generate in a week — the "no migration, searchable in the first session" wedge is an assertion, not a demo, and the kill criterion (≥80% coverage in ≤2 sessions) has already been met in the negative.

Second, and architectural: Week 3 says the company never holds the family key; Week 4 ships a public MCP server that searches the family index for Claude. One of those sentences is false, and which one decides both the BIPA defence and the COGS table.

## Weakest dimension

**Ceiling (3).** By the variant's own arithmetic a well-run version tops out at $12–25M ARR; $100M requires international scale plus the storage system-of-record the variant explicitly refuses. (Pain, Platform risk, Compounding asset and Distribution all sit at 4; of those, Platform risk is the one a builder would watch, because Apple has already restricted the exact API path this product runs on.)
