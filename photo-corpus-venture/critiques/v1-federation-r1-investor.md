# Critique — v1-federation ("Family Index, no migration"), round 1, investor lens

Written 2026-11-03. Fresh-context critic. Read in order: SCORECARD.md, variants/v1-federation.md, every file in research/ and research/assumptions/. Did not read CHANGELOG, other variants, or other critiques.

## Stance

Cold seed investor. Default is no. I care about who pays, how much, why now, the wedge, the distribution, what must be true for a $1B+ outcome, and whether the founder is fooling themselves about platform risk. Allergic to storage-company economics (this variant avoids that, credit where due), to consumer products that need behaviour change (this one needs a *second person's* behaviour change), and to small channels dressed up as markets (the organizer channel and the Claude connector are both small channels). Ceiling, Distribution and Platform risk are scored hardest.

## Most damaging objection

**Google Photos is already installed on the iPhone side of mixed households, with full-library access, Partner Sharing and face groups — so the "only party that can sit on both sides" position does not exist, and the $40/yr second bill is competing with $0.** Research/platform-access.md Q2 documents Google Photos as a full-library iOS backup app; research/customers.md Q1(c) and research/trades-and-reddit.md Part B document that when a family asks "how do we merge our libraries", the answer given every time, by every commenter, is Partner Sharing at no cost, and that in 156 posts and 178 comments nobody reports paying anyone for this job. Google's remaining gaps (one partner, private face labels) are a product decision, not a platform barrier, and Apple's gap (no People across Shared Library) is one WWDC slide. If the founder cannot name what a mixed family gets for $40/yr that two Google Photos installs plus Partner Sharing do not give them today — and show a family that has paid for it — the conversation ends.

## Weakest dimension

**Ceiling (2).** The variant tops out, by its own arithmetic, at $12–25M ARR. The only route to $100M it offers is "international scale plus a second revenue line (organizer seat or storage system-of-record) that v1 explicitly refuses", and the international pool it cites was withdrawn by the research. A seed investor needs a believable path to $100M ARR; this document argues against one.

## Unknowns to research before the next round

1. What share of US multi-person households contain at least one iPhone and at least one Android phone, from a named nat-rep survey or carrier family-plan data (Consumer Intelligence, YouGov, Comscore), rather than the independence-with-haircut estimate in sizing.md row 19?
2. What share of iPhone users in the US have the Google Photos app installed with backup on (i.e., how many "mixed households" are already served on both sides by Google)?
3. Does iCloud Shared Photo Library on iOS 27 sync People names and face clusters across participants — any 2025–2026 Apple documentation, developer-forum thread or hands-on report?
4. Has any third-party iOS 27 app shipped a full-library on-device CLIP/face index via BGContinuedProcessingTask, and what measured completion times, expiry/kill rates and maximum runtimes do developers report (including whether the `continued-processing.inference` entitlement is grantable)?
5. What invite-to-install conversion and time-to-second-member figures have family-sharing apps outside the newborn wedge published or disclosed (Tinybeans+ Family 6-account tier uptake in its FY26 annual report; Google Partner Sharing adoption; Apple Shared Library adoption)?

## Three questions for the founder

1. Pick twenty mixed-platform families you know. For each: did the relative who is *not* the buyer install, grant full Photos access and finish the index within seven days? What did you have to say to make that happen, and what did the family do before you existed?
2. A mixed family can install Google Photos on the iPhone today, turn on Partner Sharing, and get merged search and face groups for $0. Tell me the specific query or outcome that family gets for $40/yr from you and not from that — and whether anyone has paid you for it yet.
3. Your own ceiling is $12–25M ARR. Which second revenue line gets this to $100M, why is it not in v1, and what would make you decide to put the system-of-record (and its storage economics) back in?
