# GRAVEYARD

Every rejected variant, with the reason it died. The founder should see the search, not just the answer.

| Variant | Round killed | Cause of death | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standalone voice photo search/editing app | pre-v0 (founder); reason corrected round 0 | Voice *search* is native (Siri AI iOS 27, 2026-09-14; Google Ask Photos / "Help me edit" 2025-11). Voice *editing* via Siri is unproven in Apple's primary text. The durable reason to reject is distribution, not feature parity: platform search is free and preinstalled | research/competitive.md Q1–Q2, §3 |
| v0 as written (migrate everything out, then family archive + BYO-AI) | 0 | Scored 39/100. Both wedges require full-library migration before any value; no shipping app has made that unattended on iOS; nobody found paying for the merge itself; Apple's iOS 27 Foundation Models framework removes the need to migrate for BYO-AI | critiques/v0-r0-*.md; research/assumptions/A2.md, A3.md, A4.md |
| v1-federation — family index, no migration | 1 | 43/100. Pain 3 and Ceiling 3: in 156 Reddit posts and 178 comments nobody pays or is told to pay to merge libraries; Google Photos already sits on the iPhone side of mixed households with partner sharing and face groups; the Android index was deferred, so the one household it claims to serve cannot enroll. **Salvaged:** the on-device, index-only engine (86% GM, no migration) is carried into `variants/current.md` as the digital-ingest path | critiques/v1-federation-r1-*.md; research/ios-feasibility.md |
| v1-estate — bereavement event wedge | 1 | 39/100. Tukios already gives 10,000+ funeral homes a text/email link that collects relatives' phone photos for the tribute video, which was the free wedge; the Apple Data & Privacy export's contents are unverified; most decedents set no Legacy Contact; a $149 one-time product needs ~1M estates/yr for $100M. **Salvaged:** the "tribute pack from the living relatives' phones" is a module, not a company | critiques/v1-estate-r1-*.md; research/estate.md |
| v1-trades — camera-roll job memory for small contractors | 1 | 47/100 (tied), lost on mean and structure. CompanyCam ships GPS-matched camera-roll import by address (Magic Uploader), text scanning and an MCP server, and raised $415M; 20–25 of a contractor's 30 daily photos arrive from techs over iMessage and are not in PhotoKit; $12/mo pricing caps the US business at $17–34M. **Salvaged:** the dated "evidence packet" export is a feature idea for any archive | critiques/v1-trades-r1-*.md; research/trades-and-reddit.md A2, A4 |
| v2-prints — print-first family archive | 2 | 45/100. Customers tag 0.10 times per scan and pay for backup, not naming (Photomyne, the only audited P&L: ~402k payers, flat-to-down, marketing 64–68% of revenue). Ancestry sells the scan at $0.49/photo as a loss leader. Mail-in partners scan fronts only. Honest ceiling $25–45M US. **Salvaged:** prints as an ingest path; naming must be prompted by a person (a relative or an interviewer), never left to self-serve tagging | critiques/v2-prints-r2-*.md; research/digitization-market.md |
| v2-genealogy — photo layer for family historians | 2 | 45/100. The ~3–4.8M paying pool is flat and lives inside Ancestry and MyHeritage, which ship the features, own the tree and closed their APIs. FamilySearch write access is partner-gated. Honest ceiling $8–30M. **Salvaged:** GEDCOM import as a people graph; deceased relatives as the consent-safe auto-name set | critiques/v2-genealogy-r2-*.md; research/genealogy-market.md |
| v2-grandparent — newborn/grandparent loop + archive | 2 | 41/100. FamilyAlbum's loop depends on free unlimited storage of originals at ~5% segment margin; grandparents do not pay (4.3% follower-pay rate); Tinybeans' archive pivot left its core paid base flat; infant face recognition is 30.7% TAR at 0–6 months. Research file's own base case is $5–10M. **Salvaged:** no-install browser view for relatives; the parent as single payer | critiques/v2-grandparent-r2-*.md; research/grandparent-loop.md |
| v2-sdk — memory-product ingestion + curation SDK | 2 | 35/100, the lowest of any variant. Buyers with volume already built curation on Apple's free Vision aesthetics API or in-house; iOS allows no cross-app shared index; per-order pricing sits inside an 11.6% EBIT margin. Honest ceiling $5–15M. **Salvaged:** nothing structural; confirms the engine is a commodity and the value must sit in the workflow and the human-confirmed data | critiques/v2-sdk-r2-*.md; research/memory-product-sdk.md |
| v3-gift — gifted photo memoir with an AI interviewer | 3 | 43/100. A feature of the category leader's shipped product: Storyworth already phones elders with follow-up questions and uses photos as prompts; Remento and Memory Murals cover the photo-anchored and voice-tagged parts. At the category's $89–99 price the variant's contribution is $23–33. Largest sourced revenue in the category: Remento >$7M. Honest ceiling $2–24M. **Salvaged:** names spoken by a prompted elder need no face template; nothing in the build waits on Apple or Google | critiques/v3-gift-r3-*.md; research/memoir-gift-market.md |
| v3-journal — printed family journal from every phone | 3 | 41/100. Famileo sells it in the US today with ~110 iOS ratings; the US category is ~7,500 families; unit cost roughly equals price at public print and postage rates; frames do the job for a one-time price. Honest ceiling $4–16M. **Salvaged:** scoping on-device work to one month of photos avoids the long indexing session | critiques/v3-journal-r3-*.md; research/family-artifact-market.md |
| v3-care — legacy program for senior living and hospice | 3 | 41/100. Market price for the deliverable (LifeBio ~$9–18 per person per year) is below the variant's cost; the dominant engagement platform ships Life Story with AI; no vendor in the category has reached $10M. Honest ceiling $3–36M. **Salvaged:** move-in week as a trigger; the family link as the only step asked of relatives | critiques/v3-care-r3-*.md; research/institutional-legacy.md |
