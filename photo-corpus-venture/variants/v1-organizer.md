## One line

A Mac-first workbench that lets a professional photo organizer ingest a family's scattered sources (Google Takeout zips with sidecar repair, the client's Mac Photos library with face regions, drives, phones, scanner output), dedupe them, confirm who-is-who with the client on a review screen, and deliver a clean library back into Apple Photos / Google Photos / XMP — or into an optional hosted family vault — in hours instead of a ">20 hours" hand job [sourced: TPM member survey of ~250, thephotomanagers.com 2026-08-25, via research/assumptions/A3.md item 7]; the family pays the organizer, the organizer pays per seat and per project.

## Wedge (first use case adopted without changing habits; the first 10 minutes; day-1 value for ONE user; day-7 value)

**First use case, no habit change.** The organizer already (a) requests the client's Google Takeout, (b) sits at a Mac with the client's Apple Photos library or an iCloud.com export, (c) plugs in old drives and scanner output, and (d) spends hours deduping and naming people. The workbench is where those files land instead of a Finder folder. Nothing is asked of the family that is not asked today; the migration step the round-0 critics called the killer (A2.md) is done by the person who already does it, on macOS, the one platform where originals, albums and named faces with regions are all recoverable [sourced: osxphotos API/CLI docs via platform-access.md Q3; A2.md A2c].

**The first 10 minutes (one organizer, one client project).**
1. New project → drag in `takeout-*.zip` (up to 50 GB each, split archives expected [sourced: support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190 via platform-access.md Q4]) and point at the client's `.photoslibrary` on the Mac. The app unpacks, matches JSON sidecars to media including the truncated `.supplemental-metadata.json` and `(1)` variants (immich-go's matching behaviour reimplemented; AGPL code is not linked), reads `Photos.sqlite` for assets, albums, edit/original pairs and `face_info` regions with person names [sourced: platform-access.md Q3].
2. A live ledger shows every source item accounted for: imported / exact duplicate / near-duplicate pair for review / unreadable. Exact-hash and perceptual dedupe start immediately; faces and embeddings run in the background on the Mac (SigLIP-class ~60 img/s on Apple Silicon via LiteRT [sourced-secondary: huggingface.co Kairoi-LLC via research/unit-economics.md Q3]; face pipeline ~10 img/s CPU [sourced-secondary: winnow wiki via unit-economics.md Q3] → a 50k-item project is embedded in ~15 min and faced in ~1.4 h [estimated: 50,000 ÷ 60 ÷ 60; 50,000 ÷ 10 ÷ 3,600]).
3. By minute ten the organizer sees the dedupe queue and the first People clusters, pre-named wherever Apple Photos already had names.

**Day-1 value for ONE user (the organizer).** The machine-able part of a ">20 hours" project — unzip, sidecar repair, metadata normalisation, exact/near dedupe, carrying Apple names across, first-pass clustering — collapses to wall-clock that runs unattended. What share of the 20 hours that is: [unknown — no time-and-motion study exists; the 4-week demo measures it on one real project]. The honest claim today is "hours of your touch time become minutes of review," not a number. Against the alternatives the organizer has now: PowerPhotos merges Apple libraries but not Takeout; immich-go repairs Takeout but needs a terminal and an Immich server; Mylio's duplicate finder "misses a lot"; FOREVER Historian is Windows-only [sources above].

**Day-7 value.** The client-review screen: the organizer sends the family a link; relatives name the remaining clusters, flag the deceased, and (for living people) e-sign the consent the organizer needs anyway (see Platform risk). Voice notes recorded in the session attach to assets. The deliverable goes back where the family lives: an Apple Photos import with albums and names as keywords, a Google Photos upload with albums through the still-available `photoslibrary.appendonly` scope [sourced: developers.google.com/photos/support/updates via platform-access.md Q4], and XMP sidecars with `PersonInImage` + MWG regions that digiKam/Lightroom/exiftool read [sourced: research/ios-feasibility.md Q9]. Honest limit: neither Apple Photos nor Google Photos rebuilds People albums from XMP — "portable names, not portable People albums" [sourced: osxphotos import docs via ios-feasibility.md Q9]. The optional family vault (hosted index + previews + MCP) is offered at the hand-off, when the family is already paying the organizer.

## Why now (dated changes since 2024, each tagged)

1. **2025-03-31: Google removed self-serve library read (`photoslibrary.readonly`)**; Takeout and the 2,000-item Picker are the only bulk routes, so every organizer with a Google client now does Takeout by hand, and the Picker strips GPS [sourced: developers.google.com/photos/support/updates; picker docs via platform-access.md Q4; research/assumptions/A1.md holds, 0.75]. Upload *into* a client's Google account remains possible (`appendonly`) [same source].
2. **2025-03-04: Mylio's CEO repriced the product around human configuration** — Individual $240/yr with a guided setup session; Photos+ raised to $119.99 (2025); Concierge via Certified Photo Managers $299 (page also shows $450) — and **Mylio, FOREVER, Vivid-Pix and Photomyne are absent from TPM's 2026 sponsor list after sponsoring 2025** [sourced: inspire.mylio.com 2025-03-04; support.mylio.com 2026-09-25; thephotomanagers.com 2025/2026 conference pages via pro-channel.md Q1, Q3]. Mylio's blog has posted only how-tos since (latest 2026-01-06; no product, pricing or partnership news) [sourced: https://inspire.mylio.com/, fetched 2026-11-03]. The incumbent closest to this channel is monetising complexity and has stepped back from the channel.
3. **2026-04: TPM's own conference put "Humans, AI, and the Future of Photo Management" on the main stage**, and its 2026-07-07 post says "professional photo managers use AI every day"; the association now sells vendors a "$1,500 Live Member Focus Group" ("what they wish existed, what would make them switch") [sourced: thephotomanagers.com 2026-conference and prospectus 2026-09-11 via pro-channel.md Q1, Q7].
4. **2025-07-30: Ancestry acquired iMemories** (1M+ families), turning scanning output into a genealogy funnel; the post-scan consolidation step stays unserved by every scanner [sourced: ancestry.com 2025-07-30 via pro-channel.md Q5].
5. **Aug 2026: G.T. v. Samsung (7th Cir.)** — face templates computed and kept on the user's device, which the company cannot access, are outside BIPA's "possess/collect"; this is the legal reason to compute faces on the organizer's Mac rather than a server [sourced: Mayer Brown 2026-08-20 via research/face-regulation.md Q1].
6. **2026-10-07: Immich v3.3.0 shipped cross-user people sharing for free** after a 2.5-year thread — proof the "merge identities across sources" job is wanted, delivered only for self-hosters [sourced: github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v3.3.0 via A3.md Against #3].
7. **Indexing cost collapse**: a full 22,000-image caption pass is $0.69 batch on Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite, embeddings and faces are cents, all on 2026-11-03 price pages [estimated: unit-economics.md Q3]; AdaFace (MIT) is at parity with the non-commercial InsightFace pack [sourced: AdaFace README via research/ai-why-now.md Q3].
8. **Adjacent photo pros now pay per seat for AI that cuts their hours**: Narrative Select $10–60/mo (annual) with "thousands of photographers" and a claimed "253 hours a year" saved; Imagen AI "US$0.05 / photo" plus "US$7/monthly base fee", or $129/mo unlimited [sourced: https://narrative.so/pricing and https://imagen-ai.com/pricing/, fetched 2026-11-03]. Photographers, not organizers — but it is a dated, working price model for "sell the engine to the pro".
9. Counter-signal: osxphotos broke four times on macOS 26/27 and was repaired within 1–8 days each time (#2165, #2207 closed 2026-09-20; #2237 2026-09-27); one AMFI issue open since 2026-07-18 [sourced: A2.md Against #6]. The Mac path is reverse-engineered and must be owned every September.

## Product (core loop; explicitly NOT in v1)

**Core loop (per client project, on the organizer's Mac).**
1. **Sources**: Takeout zips; `.photoslibrary` (Photos.sqlite reader: assets, albums, originals + edits, face regions, person names); iCloud.com batch downloads (1,000 per batch [sourced: support.apple.com/111762 via research/estate.md Q2]); external drives and folders; scanner watch-folders (EverPresent/ScanCafe/Legacybox deliveries); phone via Image Capture. No iOS app.
2. **Ingest + repair**: sidecar matching, EXIF/XMP normalisation from Takeout JSON, Live Photo pairing, HEIC/JPEG re-encode detection, a per-item provenance ledger (every input item → one output decision).
3. **Dedupe**: exact hash → perceptual → edit/original and resolution-variant pairing; a review queue for ambiguous pairs; rules the organizer sets once per project (keep original + latest edit, keep highest resolution).
4. **Assisted identity**: on-device face detection + AdaFace embeddings + clustering; Apple names seeded from the library; cross-source merge *suggestions* only (child→adult across decades is an open research problem; adult↔aged-adult ~98% on AgeDB-30) [sourced: ai-why-now.md Q3, §6]; the organizer confirms.
5. **Client review screen** (web): name clusters, mark deceased, e-sign consent for living named people, add voice/text notes, resolve "which is the lake house" questions. Previews only; no templates leave the Mac.
6. **Dating/OCR**: Vision OCR of print backs and captions on the Mac; date inference from scan batches; place from EXIF where present.
7. **Deliver**: (a) Apple Photos import with albums + name-keywords; (b) Google Photos upload with albums (`appendonly`); (c) folder tree + XMP sidecars (PersonInImage, MWG regions) on a drive; (d) optional hosted family vault: index + previews + notes, originals optional, MCP endpoint (Claude Free custom connector install link [sourced: claude.com/docs via research/assumptions/A6.md For #1]).

**Explicitly NOT in v1.** An iOS migration app (A2b broken in practice); automatic cross-generation face matching; storing originals by default; server-side face matching for any client; naming living people without recorded consent; Windows (organizer Windows share [unknown]; FOREVER Historian being Windows-only suggests it is not small); scanning hardware control; print/photo-book ordering; a consumer self-serve tier.

## Platform risk (the obvious feature Apple/Google/incumbent could ship; why this survives or doesn't)

- **Apple/Google will not build an organizer workbench**; their risk is indirect: a lossless cross-account merge in Photos (A4 clause (a) holds today, no roadmap signal; MacRumors iOS 27 roundup: "no changes to ... library merging, duplicates, or People/faces") [sourced: research/assumptions/A4.md For #1] would remove the Apple→Apple half of the job but not Takeout, drives, prints or Google→Apple. Apple's free "Transfer to Google Photos" and Google's free transfer to iCloud already exist and drop faces, shared albums, Live pairing and originals [sourced: platform-access.md Q5] — they are inputs to the workbench, not substitutes.
- **The real incumbent is Mylio** (41 staff, ~$3M est. revenue, ships monthly, raised prices, CEO says the product "requires configuration both at the beginning and on an ongoing basis") [sourced-secondary: getlatka.com; sourced: inspire.mylio.com 2025-03-04 via pro-channel.md Q3]. A Mylio release with Takeout sidecar repair, a better duplicate finder and a client-review link is the obvious slide. Survival argument: Mylio sells the *destination* (its catalog) and has stepped back from the channel; this product is destination-neutral (Apple, Google, XMP, vault) and is sold as a project tool. Honest: Mylio could do it in two releases; the moat is time and channel trust, not technology.
- **Free assembly**: a technical organizer can chain osxphotos + immich-go + Immich 3.3 (cross-user people, free) [sourced: A3.md Against #3]. The 90%+ women, 40+ membership that finds Mylio "complicated" [sourced: prospectus; arrangewanderfocus via pro-channel.md Q1–Q2] is not that organizer, but the ceiling price is bounded by "what a Python-literate competitor organizer charges".
- **Apple's Photos.sqlite is undocumented**; encryption of the library database would remove the face-region path (A2 flip condition) [sourced: A2.md]. Fallback: PhotoKit on macOS (no People) + iCloud.com exports.
- **Google could further restrict Takeout or the `appendonly` upload**; Google's own DMA report says no Takeout changes since 2025-03-06 [sourced: platform-access.md Q4].
- **Biometric regulation is a design constraint the channel already carries.** Templates are computed and kept on the organizer's Mac (Samsung defence) and deleted at project close (TX ≤1 yr, IL ≤3 yr, CO 24 mo) [sourced: face-regulation.md Q1–Q3, Q7]; the vault stores names and MWG rectangles, not face geometry (whether rectangles plus a name are "biometric" is [unknown]; no case found). BIPA 15(b) requires the *subject's* written release, not the uploader's; the client-review screen captures an electronic signature per living named person or a deceased flag (MyHeritage pattern: auto-name only the dead) [sourced: face-regulation.md Q1, Q6]. Organizers in IL/TX/WA and the EU/UK sister bodies get server-side features off by default [face-regulation.md Q7 item 5]. The organizer is a "private entity" under BIPA whenever they tag faces in any tool today; recorded consent is a selling point, and the residual (an Illinois state court rejecting Samsung reasoning; Apple's iCloud subclass at trial) stays [sourced: face-regulation.md Q1].

## Compounding asset

- **Per organizer**: project templates, dedupe rules, the per-item provenance ledgers for every past client (the audit trail they show a family), and consent records — re-creatable only by redoing projects. Moderate switching cost.
- **Per family**: confirmed identities, voice notes, OCR'd dates on prints, and the dedupe decisions; exported by design into Apple/Google/XMP, so the family's switching cost is deliberately low unless they take the vault.
- **Company-level**: a growing corpus of Takeout/Apple-export edge cases (sidecar variants, split-zip pairings, HEIC/JPEG re-encodes, iCloud.com "Most Compatible" downsizes) as a regression suite — the same asset immich-go and osxphotos accumulate in public [sourced: github.com/simulot/immich-go; A2.md], so it is a lead, not a lock.
- **Channel-level**: certified organizers trained on the tool; the deliverable carries the vault offer; vault families compound with Backblaze-grade retention *if* the vault is the system of record, which v1 makes optional [sourced: unit-economics.md Q6].
- Honest: the index is rebuildable for cents; MCP is a commodity (54,763 active servers) [sourced: research/byo-ai.md Q5]. What compounds is workflow and trust inside a 700-person community — real, and small.

## Buildability (4-week demo spec for 2 engineers; ugliest technical risk)

**Demo (macOS 27, Apple Silicon; two engineers).**
- Week 1 — Ingest: Takeout unzip + sidecar matcher (reimplement immich-go's rules in Swift/Python; immich-go is AGPL-3.0 [sourced: github.com/simulot/immich-go, fetched 2026-11-03] so it is a reference, not a dependency); Photos.sqlite reader for assets, albums, original/edit pairs, face regions and person names (osxphotos is the reference implementation; its licence was not checked this pass [unknown — verify before reuse]); provenance ledger; exact-hash dedupe.
- Week 2 — Perceptual and pair dedupe (pHash + resolution/edit-variant rules); AdaFace (MIT) via Core ML + clustering; seed names from Apple; cross-source merge suggestions with confirm/reject.
