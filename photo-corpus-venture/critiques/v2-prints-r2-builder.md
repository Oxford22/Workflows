Net: the **smallest thing that proves the core loop** is one real box of ~300 prints, scanned on one iPhone, with on-device clustering, a "who is this?" card, a no-install web link to one relative, and the answer written into XMP — about two weeks of two engineers, no cloud VLM, no Google upload, no ZIP importers. Everything else in the spec is either a second product (partner imports, dedupe map, Google/Apple merge) or an experiment whose result is unknown (OCR, dating, face clustering on scans). The variant's own kill criterion ("tag rate <0.3/scan and link completion <15% → the post-scan layer is not wanted at this price") is correct and should run before week 3.

**What breaks at 10k users.** Cost: nothing (index-only; VLM pass pennies). Support: a 50–70 cohort plus consent-flow tickets; the research prior (Photomyne: 1 support employee for 402k payers; Legacybox complaints are billing/registration) says this is manageable. Data-loss liability: the app writes into the user's own Photos library — low, provided it never deletes; the vault is optional. Legal exposure: the live risk is not BIPA damages at 10k users, it is being forced to re-architect if the ciphertext-sync theory survives trial (Doe v. Apple) — the consent ledger and on-device templates are necessary but, per the Apple court, not sufficient. Acquisition is what actually breaks: 10k free installs against a 97K-rating incumbent cost $28k at the $2.79 CPI before a single payer, and the only evidenced non-paid channel (genealogists) is a few thousand people.

## Most damaging objection

The paid layer's three differentiated outputs — handwriting OCR of the backs, face clustering on scanned prints, and the cross-relative duplicate map — are each [unknown] on real prints (ai-why-now.md Q3–Q4; ios-feasibility.md Q3; no evidence for cross-scan dedupe), and the only measured post-scan behaviour in the category is 3.3M taggings on 32.7M scans (0.10/scan) with 85% of the incumbent's MAU never subscribing (digitization-market.md Q1, Q6). So the free scanner will most likely do what Photomyne's free tier does — acquire scanners who never do the post-scan job — and the product that is supposed to convert them rests on three experiments the variant itself says could leave it as "Photomyne with better faces." If week 4 returns back-OCR under 60% and tag rate under 0.3, there is no product, only a free app competing with a $100-per-payer incumbent.

## Weakest dimension

**Distribution (3).** The one audited operator in this category spends 68% of revenue on Facebook/Google ads and has stopped adding users; paid CAC ($65–100) exceeds the max tolerable ($53) at category retention; and every non-paid channel in the variant is either unmeasured (relative loop; partner delivery-email placement, appetite [unknown]) or too small to matter (organizers, libraries, genealogists). Ceiling is also 3, but the variant already concedes it honestly; distribution is what kills this before the ceiling is ever tested.
