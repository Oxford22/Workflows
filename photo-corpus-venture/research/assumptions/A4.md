## The assumption, as stated

v0: "A4. Apple and Google will not ship cross-account family merge + open AI access themselves." Brief's expanded form: "... within 24 months, OR if they do, a startup still has a position."

The 24-month window runs to 2028-11-03 and contains two more Apple OS cycles (iOS 28 ~Sept 2027, iOS 29 ~Sept 2028) and two Google I/Os [estimated: Apple's September release cadence — iOS 26 2025-09-15, iOS 27 2026-09-14 per competitive.md Q1 — and Google's May I/O cadence, projected forward].

## Verdict

**partial** — confidence **0.60**.

Split by clause:

| Clause | Status today | Status over 24 months |
|---|---|---|
| (a) No cross-account *library merge* from Apple or Google | **holds** (primary, re-verified today) | **unknown** — no roadmap signal either way; 0 posts on it in 13 months of Google Photos blog; iOS 27 changed Shared Albums, not Shared Library |
| (b) No *open* (third-party-model) AI access to Apple/Google photo corpora | **holds** (primary); direction of travel is *inward* on both sides | **likely holds** — Google tightened API access (A1), Apple removed its ChatGPT hand-off in iOS 27 |
| (c) "If they do, a startup still has a position" | **partial** — a position exists for the *archive/merge* product; the *BYO-AI* position is thin and was already tested to failure once (Khoj) | depends on A3 |

Why not "holds": clauses (a) and (b) are forecasts, and the primary evidence can only show absence today. More importantly, the assumption sets the wrong bar. Apple and Google do not need to ship *open* AI access to erode the venture: both have shipped *closed* AI access (Gemini over Google Photos since 2025-03; Siri AI over Apple Photos since 2026-09) and are iterating on it at a pace of several releases per year. For any customer who is content with the house assistant over a single-platform library, the "open" gap is irrelevant. The venture survives A4 only on the *merge* clause, which is the one with the weakest why-now and the one Track A could not tie to a paying segment.

Why not "broken": nothing found today ships cross-account merge or exposes either corpus to a non-house model, and Apple's own developer plumbing (App Intents entity schemas; Foundation Models with Claude and Gemini as listed providers) creates a concrete, Apple-sanctioned position for a third-party photo app on iOS — one that does not require migrating data out.

## Evidence AGAINST (sought first)

### Against clause (a): are they moving toward merge?

1. **iOS 27 opened Shared Albums to non-Apple contributors at full resolution.** Apple: "It's easier for friends and family on Android or Windows to join and contribute their own photos on iCloud.com"; Shared Albums add "full-resolution sharing" [sourced: https://www.apple.com/ios/, fetched 2026-11-03]; "Access to the latest Shared Albums features requires iOS 27 or later or macOS 27 or later" [sourced: https://www.apple.com/icloud/, footnote 3, fetched 2026-11-03]; MacRumors: Android/Windows contributions, full-resolution uploads, 30-day expiry, activity log [sourced: https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/ios-27/, last timeline entry 2026-11-02, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: moderate. This is a cross-*platform* pooling step for families and shows Apple is still investing in family sharing in 2026. It is album-level forward sharing, not library merge, and People/face identity is not mentioned.
2. **Google's "family" language is appearing in AI features.** Sept 2026: "Select, brighten and share the best family portraits" with Gemini Spark, U.S. AI Pro/Ultra only [sourced: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/photos/google-photos-updates/, 2026-09-24]. Weight: weak — editing/sharing *your* photos, not merging accounts.
3. **The merge job exists and is done by hand today**, which is exactly the kind of job a platform absorbs: Mylio's concierge consolidation costs $299 ($379 with a Photos+ year) [sourced: competitive.md Q3/Q6 citing mylio.com/concierge, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: weak as a refutation (no platform signal), but it means the demand, if it exists, is visible to Apple and Google too.

### Against clause (b): are they moving toward open AI access?

4. **Google has shipped closed AI access and keeps extending it.** Gemini app ↔ Google Photos connector dated 2025-03-13 in Gemini's release notes [sourced: https://gemini.google/release-notes/, entry 2025.03.13, as read in competitive.md Q2]; Personal Intelligence uses Photos people/pet labels for generation (2026-04-16) [sourced: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/personal-intelligence-nano-banana/, 2026-04-16, via competitive.md]; Gemini Spark edits, curates and auto-builds shared albums (2026-09-04, U.S., AI Pro/Ultra) [sourced: techcrunch.com 2026-09-04, secondary, via competitive.md]; Gemini "You can connect more than 40 apps or services" via a new Customize menu (2026-10-13) [sourced: https://gemini.google/release-notes/, entry 2026.10.13, fetched 2026-11-03]; Google Photos is one of 86 apps in Gemini's connected-apps catalogue [sourced: support.google.com/gemini/table/17434654, via byo-ai.md, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: **strong against the venture, weak against the letter of A4** — "AI access" is shipped; "open" is not. Three dated Photos-AI steps in 18 months (2025-03, 2026-04, 2026-09) [estimated: count of the dated entries above] is the pace to assume for the next 24.
5. **Apple has shipped closed AI access over Photos and is building the plumbing that lets a third-party app sit *beside* Apple Photos, not replace it.** Siri AI (iOS 27, released 2026-09-14) can "Search for a photo from years ago" using personal context, with "the Spotlight index and App Toolbox" working "entirely on device" [sourced: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/, 2026-09-14, fetched 2026-11-03]. App Intents: "Entity schemas contribute your content to the Spotlight semantic index for personal context understanding" [sourced: https://developer.apple.com/ios/, fetched 2026-11-03]. Foundation Models: "The Foundation Models framework now extends to server models — like Claude, Gemini, and more — all through the same Swift API, with full support for features like tool calling and guided generation"; "Supported third-party models: Core AI Language Models, MLX Language Models, Claude, Gemini" [sourced: https://developer.apple.com/foundation-models-framework/, © 2026, fetched 2026-11-03]. Anthropic's package "conforms Claude to the framework's `LanguageModel` protocol", requires "iOS 27, macOS 27, visionOS 27, or watchOS 27 (beta)", beta; the README install snippet pins `from: "0.1.0"` (a SwiftPM floor, not a release), and the repo's tags run 0.1.0 (2026-06-08) to 0.2.3 (2026-10-07) [sourced: https://github.com/anthropics/ClaudeForFoundationModels README and /tags, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: **the strongest single finding.** On iOS 27 a third-party app can, with PhotoKit permission, run Claude or Gemini with tool calling over the user's *existing* library on-device/PCC — no export, no storage, no migration. That is "BYO-AI over your Apple photos" delivered by Apple's own framework. It does not refute A4 (Apple is not exposing *Apple Photos* to third-party models; an app is), but it removes the venture's architectural premise that BYO-AI requires moving the corpus out, and it is available to every iOS developer.
6. **Apple is moving *inward*, not opening.** Visual Intelligence's hand-off of hard questions to ChatGPT is "no longer necessary" in iOS 27 because Siri answers directly [sourced: https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/ios-27/, fetched 2026-11-03]; Apple's models were "custom-built in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models" [sourced: Apple newsroom 2026-09-14]. Weight: this cuts *for* clause (b) (no opening) but *against* the venture's framing: the one third-party-model door Apple had opened in the consumer OS was closed within two releases.

### Against clause (c): does a startup keep a position?

7. **The BYO-AI layer is commoditized.** Official MCP registry: 55,620 server names, 54,763 active [sourced: registry.modelcontextprotocol.io API, measured 2026-11-03 06:23 UTC, via byo-ai.md Q5]; 28 "immich mcp" repos, 35 "google photos mcp", 22 "apple photos mcp", nearly all created 2026, nearly all under 100 stars [sourced: GitHub search API, 2026-11-03, via byo-ai.md Q5]; PhotoPrism ships MCP in-product [sourced: byo-ai.md Q6]. Weight: strong against a BYO-AI *wedge*; neutral for the archive product.
8. **The one consumer "your corpus as a tool for any AI" company shut down citing the big labs.** Khoj closed its cloud on 2026-04-15, naming "challenges in defensibility with all the big labs coming out with similar products" [sourced: app.khoj.dev, 2026-04-15, via byo-ai.md §3]. Weight: moderate — one data point, text/notes corpus not photos, but it is a direct test of clause (c) for the BYO-AI half and it failed.
9. **Both platforms' AI features are gated today, but the gates are theirs to open.** Siri AI "rolling out in English" (6 languages), "will not be initially available in the EU on iOS", "not available for users under 13", server-side features "subject to daily usage limits", expanded access "available for a fee in the future" [sourced: https://www.apple.com/ios/, footnotes, fetched 2026-11-03]; Ask Photos "isn't available in all regions", requires opt-in to Gemini features and backed-up images [sourced: https://support.google.com/photos/answer/15318661, fetched 2026-11-03]; Spark Photos features U.S. AI Pro/Ultra only [sourced: blog.google 2026-09-24]. Weight: these gaps are the startup's current opening and will narrow over 24 months at the platforms' discretion [estimated: inference from Google's Ask Photos going from U.S. early access 2024-09-05 to "more than 100 new countries" 2025-11-11, per competitive.md Q2].

Net of the "against" column: nobody has shipped merge or open access, but (4) and (5) show both companies shipping *closed* AI access on a multi-release-per-year cadence, and (5) shows Apple giving every iOS app an official way to run Claude/Gemini over the user's existing photos. The assumption's "open" qualifier is doing too much work.

## Evidence FOR

### Clause (a): no cross-account merge, re-verified today

1. **Apple Shared Library unchanged in iOS 27.** "share photos and videos seamlessly with up to five other people"; "You can join one Shared Library"; contributed items "move out of your Personal Library and into the Shared Library"; People/faces not mentioned; article published 2026-09-17, i.e. post-iOS 27 [sourced: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT213248, 2026-09-17, fetched 2026-11-03]. iCloud marketing: "Share the photos and videos you want with up to five other people"; no merging mentioned [sourced: https://www.apple.com/icloud/, fetched 2026-11-03]. MacRumors iOS 27 roundup: "no changes to iCloud Shared Photo Library, Family Sharing, library merging, duplicates, or People/faces" [sourced: https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/ios-27/, fetched 2026-11-03].
2. **Google partner sharing unchanged.** "You can use partner sharing with one person at a time"; only backed-up photos; only Google Accounts; no merge or cross-account face groups described [sourced: https://support.google.com/photos/answer/7378858, ©2026, fetched 2026-11-03]. "Face labels are private to each account and aren't shared across accounts." [sourced: https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6128838, fetched 2026-11-03, via competitive.md Q2/Q6].
3. **Thirteen months of Google Photos announcements contain nothing on it.** The Google Photos RSS feed lists 20 posts from 2025-10-24 to 2026-10-29; 0 mention shared libraries, family sharing, partner sharing, merging accounts, other AI assistants, API or MCP; the themes are Ask Photos, editing (Nano Banana, touch-up, Moods, Remix, Video Remix), Wardrobe, Recap, Google TV [sourced: https://blog.google/products/photos/rss/, fetched 2026-11-03]. The Sept 2026 "5 updates" post is entirely editing/Wardrobe/Markup [sourced: blog.google 2026-09-24]; the June 2026 Android Drop's "share pics with iPhone users" is Quick Share/AirDrop, not Photos [sourced: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/android-drop-june-2026/, 2026-06-02, fetched 2026-11-03].
4. **No one else has shipped it either**, so there is no competitive pressure forcing the platforms' hand: Ente family plans share storage only; Ancestry Face Match is "only designed to help identify deceased people"; FamilySearch is tree-centric [sourced: competitive.md Q6, primaries fetched 2026-11-03].

### Clause (b): no open third-party AI access, re-verified today

5. **Google's Photos API page still has no entry after the 2025-03-31 removal**, and "AI, Gemini, MCP, agents" do not appear on it [sourced: https://developers.google.com/photos/support/updates, posted Sept 2024, fetched 2026-11-03]. A1 (holds, 0.75) covers the durability of this in depth [sourced: assumptions/A1.md, 2026-11-03].
6. **Gemini's release notes June–October 2026 contain no Photos-to-third-party or MCP-for-Photos entry**; the only connected-app entries are Google's own apps and the generic ">40 apps" Customize menu [sourced: https://gemini.google/release-notes/, entries 2026.06.30–2026.10.13, fetched 2026-11-03]. Gemini's connected-apps help page does not name Google Photos; it says Gemini can "search your photos and videos from supported gallery apps" and that users can "connect personal or third-party apps by linking their Model Context Protocol (MCP) server" — i.e. Google reads *in*, it does not publish Photos *out* [sourced: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13695044, ©2026, fetched 2026-11-03].
7. **OpenAI's Apps SDK / plugins page names no Google Photos, Apple Photos or iCloud connector** [sourced: https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk, fetched 2026-11-03]; no ChatGPT Photos connector exists [sourced: byo-ai.md Q4, 2026-11-03].
8. **Apple exposes third-party apps to Siri, never Apple Photos to third-party models.** Apple's iOS 27 release and ios/ pages describe Siri acting in apps and searching photos; neither mentions ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini as user-facing integrations [sourced: Apple newsroom 2026-09-14; https://www.apple.com/ios/, both fetched 2026-11-03]. Apple's `.photos` app-schema domain lets a *third-party* photo app expose `asset`, `album`, `recognizedPerson` entities to Siri; `search` and `openAsset` schemas are deprecated [sourced: developer.apple.com app-schema-domain-photos.json, fetched 2026-11-03, via byo-ai.md Q4]. Whether PhotoKit or App Intents gained any export/semantic-index API in iOS 27 is [unknown — https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photokit and /appintents returned title-only on fetch].

### Clause (c): a startup position exists

9. **Apple's own plumbing gives a third-party photo app a sanctioned seat.** `IndexedEntity` + Spotlight donation makes an app's photos semantically searchable by Siri; `EntityStringQuery` lets the app answer free-text requests from its own server; iOS/macOS 27.0+ [sourced: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/integrating-your-photo-app-with-apple-intelligence, © 2026, via byo-ai.md Q4]. Whether Siri prefers Apple Photos over a third-party library for "show me photos of X" is [unknown: no Apple statement found, per byo-ai.md].
10. **The merge gap is real and unfilled** (competitive.md Q6 verdict: "gap, not graveyard — but a thin one"), and the only current solution is a $299 human service [sourced: competitive.md Q3/Q6, fetched 2026-11-03].
11. **Trust contrast with Google**: from 2026-10-16 connected Photos data may personalize other Google services, serve ads and train gen-AI if the user opts in [sourced: ai-why-now.md §6 scorecard, citing Google policy 2026-10-16]. Weight: a position against Google only; Apple's PCC story is stronger than a startup's [sourced: ai-why-now.md §6].
12. **Assistants accept private corpora** across Claude (Free tier), Gemini (custom MCP apps, 18+, U.S., English), ChatGPT (plugins, plan gating unknown) [sourced: byo-ai.md §3 A6, primaries fetched 2026-11-03]. The channel exists; it is just not exclusive (see Against 7).

## What observable fact would flip the verdict

To **broken** (any one, from a primary source):
- Apple raises the one-Shared-Library-per-person limit, allows a participant's existing library (with People identities) to be merged rather than moved, or announces a "family library" at WWDC 2027/2028 — on https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT213248 or an Apple newsroom post.
- Google partner sharing goes above "one person at a time" or face labels become shareable across accounts — on https://support.google.com/photos/answer/7378858 or /6128838.
- Google Photos appears as a connector/plugin in ChatGPT's directory or Claude's connector list, or a `photoslibrary` read scope reappears on https://developers.google.com/photos/support/updates.
- Apple ships an App Intents/PhotoKit API that lets a *third-party model* (via Foundation Models `LanguageModel`) query the system Photos semantic index, or Siri AI gains a user-selectable third-party model over Photos.
- Customer research (A3) finds that target families are satisfied by Gemini-over-Google-Photos or Siri-over-Apple-Photos — this breaks clause (c) without the platforms shipping anything "open".

To **holds** (confidence ≥ 0.8):
- WWDC 2027 and Google I/O 2027 pass with no merge or open-access announcement (next check: 2027-06-30), *and* A3 shows a paying segment whose libraries span both platforms (so closed single-platform AI cannot serve them).

Would lower confidence toward 0.4 without flipping: a primary Apple or Google statement that cross-account library consolidation is on the roadmap; or a second consumer BYO-AI company shutting down for Khoj's reasons.
