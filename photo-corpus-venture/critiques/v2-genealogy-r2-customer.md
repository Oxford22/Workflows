# v2-genealogy — Round 2 critique, TARGET CUSTOMER lens

**Stance.** I am 64. I pay Ancestry $39.99/month for World Explorer (≈$480/yr on monthly billing; $358/yr if I switched to the 6-month plan). I have a 2,000-person tree, mirrored on FamilySearch. I have scanned 800 prints into a folder on my Mac. I want each print attached to the right person in my tree, I want my cousins to see them, and I will not have the faces of living relatives tagged by a machine without their say-so. What I weigh: would I actually do the first step; what do I give up; who do I trust; what happens if the startup dies; what this costs me versus what I do today; would I tell a peer at my society meeting. I scored Pain, Wedge and Distribution hardest, as instructed.

Read in order: SCORECARD.md; variants/v2-genealogy.md; every file in research/ including research/assumptions/; variants/current.md for what is kept. I did not read CHANGELOG, other v2 variants, or any critique.

## Most damaging objection

**The product cannot put a name on a photo where my tree lives, and that is the whole reason I would install it.** I am an Ancestry World Explorer subscriber with a FamilySearch mirror. Ancestry has no public API; its partner API was re-platformed on 2026-05-05 to serve only RootsMagic/FTM tree sync (research/genealogy-market.md Q4). FamilySearch Memories Write exists but only after "acceptance in the Solutions Program", which asks for "demonstrated technical skills and a strong business track record" (same source) — a four-week-old demo has neither, and the variant's own assumption 2 gives it six months. Apple Photos and Google Photos will not rebuild People from XMP (research/ios-feasibility.md Q9). So on the day I import my GEDCOM and point at 800 scans, what I get is clusters ranked by era that I name myself — the variant's own words: "Photomyne with a tree" (Photomyne: 0.10 tags per scan, research/digitization-market.md Q6) — followed by "a named-and-dated export the user uploads" to Ancestry, i.e. I still attach 800 photos to 800 persons in the Tree Gallery by hand, which is exactly the job I pay $480/yr for Ancestry to make tolerable and came here to get rid of. The cousin link and the "ask" layer are nice, but neither moves a single name into my tree. Until FamilySearch certification lands, the deliverable is a folder of keywords.

## Weakest dimension

**Distribution (3)**, tied with Ceiling (3). From where I sit, Ceiling is the founder's problem and the variant already concedes it; Distribution is mine: I will not find this product. The only zero-cost storefront that reaches me is the FamilySearch Solutions Gallery, which is gated on the same certification the wedge depends on and already carries Related Faces ($99/yr, face matching off FamilySearch Memories). The forum I use deletes vendor mentions. The webinar library is owned by MyHeritage, which sells the competing $49.90 Photo plan. RootsTech's booth price is unknown and the one "it worked for a small vendor" anecdote turned out to be a nonprofit's. Paid acquisition at $65–90 per payer is underwater against a 15.6-month category life. The cousin link is the only organic loop, and no company that runs one discloses its acceptance rate.

## Unknowns to research before the next round

1. **FamilySearch Solutions Program lead time and acceptance rate**: median time from application to Memories-Write certification for a new vendor, and whether any startup under two years old has been certified (search developers.familysearch.org community, partner testimonials, RootsTech "Innovation" talks: "FamilySearch Solutions Provider certification how long").
2. **MyHeritage Photo-plan take-up**: number or share of MyHeritage subscribers on the $49.90 Photo plan since Nov 2024 — the only direct comparable for "genealogists pay a second subscription for photos" (Francisco Partners sale materials, Calcalist/Globes deal coverage, MyHeritage investor decks: "MyHeritage Photo plan subscribers").
3. **Related Faces scale**: customers or revenue, and whether it reads FamilySearch Memories through a certified API or by user export — it is the closest shipping product and the gallery incumbent (relatedfaces.com about/press; RootsTech 2025/2026 exhibitor talks; "Related Faces genealogy customers").
4. **Ancestry bulk-attach path**: does any Ancestry surface (Tree Gallery, AncestryPreserve, FTM/RootsMagic sync) accept a folder of photos with a person mapping (CSV, XMP PersonInImage, filename convention) so that a named export can be attached without per-photo clicking (help.ancestry.com; FTM 2024 / RootsMagic 11.2 media-sync docs: "Ancestry upload photos tag tree person bulk CSV").
5. **Share of faces in a genealogist's scan collection that are living people**: measured on 10–20 real collections (e.g. via a FamilySearch community or society pilot), because the per-subject consent gate leaves a worked 19–46% of appearances un-named (research/face-regulation.md Q7) and the variant's "this segment is unusually tolerant because most are deceased" is untested ("family photo collection share living vs deceased subjects survey").
