Written 2026-11-03. Fresh context: read SCORECARD.md, variants/v2-prints.md, every file in research/ (incl. research/assumptions/A1–A6), and variants/current.md. No CHANGELOG, no other v2 variants, no other critiques.

## Stance

I am 58. I cleared out my mother's house in the spring and have three boxes: loose prints from the 1950s–90s, two carousels of slides, four albums with captions in my grandmother's hand. iPhone; I pay Google One because the 200 GB tier was the easy upgrade. I priced a Legacybox Family kit (~$320) and did not order. I installed Google PhotoScan, scanned maybe twenty prints at a kitchen table, and stopped. My aunt is 86 and is the last person who knows who half these people are. What I weigh: would I actually do the first step; what do I have to give up (time, the originals, my privacy, my relatives' patience); who do I trust with my mother's photos; what happens if the company folds; what does this cost me against what I do today (nothing, plus $60/yr to Google); and would I tell my sister to use it.

## The single most damaging objection

**For the customer the variant names — a box, not an album — the differentiating input never arrives.** The only things in the day-1 promise that neither PhotoScan nor a mail-in ZIP delivers are the backs (handwriting, dates) and the names. The backs exist only on the phone-scan path, which for my three boxes is roughly 2,000 prints photographed twice at the kitchen table — the behaviour I already abandoned after twenty, and the behaviour Photomyne's own filing says 85% of its monthly users never convert on and tag at one in ten scans (research/digitization-market.md Q1, Q6). The variant's answer for "big boxes" is a mail-in partner, and the partner it names (iMemories/Ancestry) scans "only the front sides" (research/digitization-market.md Q3), so the grandmother's captions are gone before the app ever sees them. The names come from relatives answering links at a rate no family product has ever disclosed (research/grandparent-loop.md Q4), filtered through a per-subject e-signature for anyone living (research/face-regulation.md Q7 rule 2). What survives all that and lands in my iPhone is a JPEG whose embedded `PersonInImage` Apple Photos ignores (research/ios-feasibility.md Q9). So the first decision the product forces — scan it myself or mail it — strips out its own reason to exist, and what I'd recommend to my sister is the free app plus a $320 Legacybox kit, which is what I was already going to do.

## Unknowns research could resolve before the next round

1. **Box-scale behaviour in phone scanning:** what is the median number of scans per converting user at Photomyne/PhotoScan, and what share of users ever exceed 500 scans? (Search: "Photomyne scans per user cohort" / "Photomyne annual report מספר סריקות למשתמש" / any PhotoScan usage study.)
2. **Does a mail-in scanner capture backs?** Which of ScanMyPhotos, EverPresent, ScanCafe, Legacybox, Memories Renewed offer back-side scanning, at what per-print price, and what share of orders take it? (iMemories: fronts only, sourced.)
3. **Will the names be visible at all?** Device test: import a JPEG with embedded IPTC Keywords + XMP `PersonInImage` into iOS 27 Photos — are keywords searchable in the Photos app? Upload the same file via Google Photos `appendonly` — does Google Photos search find the embedded caption/keywords? (Search: "iOS Photos search IPTC keywords imported JPEG", "Google Photos API upload preserves XMP description search".)
4. **Handwriting on photo paper:** any published accuracy for Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite / Claude Haiku 5.5 / PP-OCRv5 on faded cursive or ballpoint on photographic verso (historical handwriting benchmarks: IAM-HistDB, READ, ICDAR HTR competitions; "photo verso handwriting OCR dataset").
5. **Relative response rate for any memory product:** Storyworth weekly-question response rate, Tukios tribute-upload-link completion, FamilyAlbum relatives-per-family or invite acceptance, Tinybeans invite acceptance — one disclosed number would anchor assumption 4 (≥15% links answered in 7 days). (Search: "Storyworth response rate", "Tukios family upload link completion rate", "FamilyAlbum 招待 承認率".)

## Three questions for the founder

1. Have you personally scanned one full box — at least 1,000 loose prints, front and back — with the flow in §Wedge, and timed it? At what print count did you want to stop, and what did you do with the slides and album pages?
2. After I pay $59.99 and the AI pass runs, open my iPhone's Photos app: what exactly do I see that I would not see after a free PhotoScan session? If the answer is "search for a keyword", show me that working on iOS 27 today, and show me what my sister on Android sees in Google Photos.
3. If you shut the company in 18 months, what do I hold? The JPEGs are in my library — fine. Where do my aunt's identifications, the "which cousin holds which original" map, the decade confirmations and the consent records live, and in what file can I take them to digiKam, Apple or Google?
