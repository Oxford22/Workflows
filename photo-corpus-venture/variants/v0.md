# v0 — Seed idea (as received, 2026-11-03)

## One line
A user-owned photo/media corpus with AI on top: the user's photos migrated out of Apple/Google
into a store they control, fully indexed (embeddings, faces, OCR, VLM), queryable by voice/text
and by any AI model.

## Candidate wedges (as received)
1. **Family archive** — merge multiple relatives' libraries across iCloud/Google/scanned prints,
   dedupe, cluster faces across generations, attach voice notes, ownership that outlives any one account.
2. **BYO-AI interface** — expose the corpus as an MCP/tool endpoint so Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini can
   search it. Claimed to matter because Google removed third-party read access to Photos libraries
   in March 2025. [unverified at v0 — see research/platform-access.md]

## Already rejected (by founder, before v0)
- Standalone voice photo search/editing app — native in iOS 27 Siri AI and Google Ask Photos. [unverified at v0]

## Known killers (founder's list)
- Migration friction
- iOS background-upload limits
- Apple/Google shipping the obvious feature
- "Storage company" economics and trust burden

## Customer
Unspecified. Implied: consumers with large multi-platform photo libraries; families.

## Business model
Unspecified. Implied: consumer subscription.

## Load-bearing assumptions (to falsify first)
- A1. Google actually removed third-party read access to Photos libraries (March 2025) and this is durable.
- A2. Apple PhotoKit / iOS background constraints make full-library export to a third party feasible at all.
- A3. There exists a customer segment for whom "merged multi-relative archive" is an acute, paid-for pain.
- A4. Apple and Google will not ship cross-account family merge + open AI access themselves.
- A5. Unit economics of storage + embedding + VLM per user can clear a consumer price point.
- A6. Major assistants will let users attach a private corpus as a tool (MCP or equivalent) with low friction.

## Prior art to verify
Apple Photos + Siri AI (iOS 27), Google Ask Photos / Gemini, Ente, Immich, PhotoPrism, Mylio,
Amazon Photos, CompanyCam, Google Takeout, Apple Data Transfer, Data Transfer Initiative, EU DMA portability.
