## Stance

I am 34, first baby, iPhone. My parents are on Android, my in-laws on iPhone. Baby photos go out over an iMessage thread (in-laws) and a WhatsApp group (my side). I tried Tinybeans, let the trial lapse, pay iCloud+ 200 GB ($2.99/mo [research/unit-economics.md Q5]), and will not put the baby on Instagram. I am scoring: would I do step one, what do I give up, whom do I trust, what happens if you die, what it costs against what I do now, and whether I would tell a friend. Pain, Wedge and Distribution are scored hardest.

## Most damaging objection

**You are asking me to pay FamilyAlbum's price for a feed that keeps less than FamilyAlbum's free tier, in order to solve a problem that is yours and not mine — and what I actually use today costs nothing.**

Concretely:

- The headline benefit is "nothing re-uploaded, nothing stored by us but previews". That is your storage bill (grandparent-loop.md Q6). I have never experienced the second upload as a cost; to me a second copy held by someone else is a backup. Your own assumption 1 names "it doesn't keep my originals" as the exit reason you fear.
- Family is $59/yr — set "head-on" at FamilyAlbum Premium Family's $59. FamilyAlbum's free tier already gives unlimited uploads and permanent history, and grandparents can "view through a browser without an account" (grandparent-loop.md Q7, S13). Your free tier gives a 30-day feed. So on day 31 my parents lose the newborn photos unless I pay, on top of the $2.99/mo I already pay Apple.
- My real baseline is not FamilyAlbum at all. It is an iMessage thread and a WhatsApp group: $0, end-to-end encrypted, nobody installs anything, and the replies land where the rest of the family conversation already lives. Since 2026-06-08 my Android parents can also join an iCloud Shared Album at full resolution (customers.md Q1(c)). The variant never compares itself to this, and the research has one table cell on it ("WhatsApp/text", $0).
- The "find" step does not match the job. In the first months my camera roll is the baby; Apple already gives me her People album (the variant concedes this). What takes my time is choosing which 3 of 14 are fit for my father-in-law — and "14 photos of Mia today — send?" is one tap away from sending the bath photo and the one where I am nursing. The two most-engaged family posts in the whole Reddit sample are parents worried about baby bath photos being flagged by a cloud (trades-and-reddit.md Part B, scores 58 and 53). The only infant-face number in the research is 30.7% for 0–6 months.
- The part that would be genuinely new — "show me her mother at the same age" across three generations — only exists after both grandparent households sign a biometric consent screen, scan their albums and name faces. The one dataset on that behaviour shows 0.10 tags per scan (digitization-market.md Q6), and the plan charges those grandparents $99/yr when the research says they are the people who will not pay.

I let a Tinybeans trial lapse. Nothing in the first ten minutes here changes the reason I did.

## Weakest dimension

**Ceiling (3), tied with Platform risk (3).** Ceiling is the dimension this round was meant to fix, and the variant's own honest number is $25–45M US against a research base case of $5–10M for an index-only entrant on this loop. From the customer chair the two scores that explain the low ceiling are Pain and Wedge at 4: the paid delta over free alternatives is thin, so both the paid rate and the enrolment rate in the ceiling chain are at the optimistic end.

## Unknowns research could resolve before the next round

1. What share of US parents of children under 2 share baby photos mainly through iMessage/WhatsApp group threads or iCloud Shared Albums versus a dedicated app (FamilyAlbum, Tinybeans), and what reasons do people who quit a dedicated app give? (Look for BabyCenter, What to Expect, Pew, or the Tinybeans "How Moms share & save memories" April 2025 n=600 survey.)
2. What is FamilyAlbum's Premium subscriber count or paid rate, its North America family count, and has any FamilyAlbum release since v23.0.0 (2026-10-05 "Memories screen") added automatic photo selection or AI search? (MIXI Q3/Q4 FY2026 and Q2 FY2027 results decks; US App Store version history.)
3. What precision and recall do open face models (AdaFace, ArcFace) reach when clustering one infant aged 0–12 months across photos taken days to weeks apart, as opposed to the longitudinal verification in Hossain et al., IJCB 2025? (Full text of that paper; any infant re-identification or same-session clustering benchmark.)
4. When a newly installed iOS photo app requests library access, what share of users choose "Limit Access" over "Full Access", and does it differ for parenting-category apps? (Apple statements, developer analytics posts, academic permission studies since iOS 14.)
5. Do grandparents ever pay in this category: what share of FamilyAlbum follower-billing payers are grandparents, and how many subscribers took Tinybeans+ Family (6 accounts) or Legacy? (MIXI decks after Q1 FY2026; Tinybeans FY26 annual report and Q1 FY27 Appendix 4C, both ASX-gated on 2026-11-03.)
