## The assumption, as stated

v0: "A6. Major assistants will let users attach a private corpus as a tool (MCP or equivalent) with low friction." Brief's expanded form adds the test that matters for the venture: "...with low enough friction that it can be a wedge rather than a power-user feature."

Split into the three claims it actually makes:

- (a) **Availability**: each of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini lets an ordinary consumer account attach an arbitrary remote MCP server.
- (b) **Friction**: the attach flow is short, works where consumers are (phone), and carries no gate (plan, country, age, admin, developer mode) that excludes most of them.
- (c) **Wedge**: the result is something a mainstream consumer would do as the *reason* to adopt a product, not a feature power users bolt on afterwards.

## Verdict

**partial** — confidence **0.60**.

| Clause | Claude | ChatGPT | Gemini | Apple Siri / MS consumer Copilot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Availability to a consumer account | **holds** — Free plan, 1 custom connector [sourced: claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted.md, fetched 2026-11-03] | **holds on paper** — official flow exists [sourced: developers.openai.com/plugins/deploy/connect-chatgpt.md, fetched 2026-11-03]; plan gate `[unknown]` on any OpenAI page; every secondary says paid-only | **holds with gates** — 18+, US, English, personal account, Keep Activity on [sourced: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137, fetched 2026-11-03] | **broken** — no path [sourced: byo-ai.md Q1, Apple and Microsoft primaries fetched 2026-11-03] |
| (b) Low friction | **mostly holds** — 5 documented steps or a prefilled install link; desktop/web add, mobile use; per-tool approval prompts | **broken for consumers** — web-only, "Work" tab, `@`-invocation, risk warning, likely paid plan + Developer Mode (secondary) | **partial** — 6 steps on a desktop browser; then mobile use; US/English only | n/a |
| (c) Wedge rather than power-user feature | **unproven** — vendor frames custom connectors as developer testing path; labelled "Custom", never suggested in-product | **broken** — the flow lives in developer docs and a "personal plugin" | **unproven-to-broken** — the add path is in "Connected Apps > Custom apps" with a "Google does not control, monitor, or secure" warning | broken |

Why not "holds": clause (c) has no supporting evidence anywhere. All three vendors document the custom-server path as a developer or power-user tool (Anthropic's own instruction for *testing* a server is "add it to your own Claude account as a custom connector"; OpenAI's flow produces a "personal plugin" usable from the Work tab; Google routes it through "Gemini Developer Mode" and a "Custom apps" settings sub-section). The two assistants with published nine-figure user counts gate or hide the path: Gemini to US/18+/English/desktop-add, ChatGPT (per every reachable secondary) to paid plans, web, Developer Mode. Apple and Microsoft consumer surfaces have no path at all. No page fetched states how many consumers have ever added a custom connector on any assistant.

Why not "broken": clause (a) is now true on Claude for Free accounts with a one-click prefilled install link, and true on Gemini for a US adult with no subscription requirement stated. That is materially better than the 2025 picture and is primary-sourced. A consumer product can ship a working "ask Claude/Gemini about my photos" path today.

## Evidence AGAINST (sought first)

### Against (a)/(b) — ChatGPT, the largest assistant, does not document a consumer path and secondaries say it is gated

1. **OpenAI's own pages never state which consumer plans can add a custom MCP server, and the official flow runs through developer tooling.** The connect page: "Go to ChatGPT Plugins", "Select the plus button, then Add custom MCP server", "Enter a user-facing name and description", enter the URL "including the `/mcp` path", "Configure authentication, review the risk warning, and select I understand and want to continue", "Select Create as a plugin", "Review the tools and metadata discovered from the server"; the only eligibility sentence is "Account and workspace policies apply to adding and using custom MCP servers" [sourced: https://developers.openai.com/plugins/deploy/connect-chatgpt.md, undated, fetched 2026-11-03]. The quickstart: "This tutorial creates a personal plugin by connecting an MCP server"; after install, "At the top of the homepage, switch the tab from Chat to Work", "Start a new Work chat", "type @ and select your plugin to invoke it directly" [sourced: https://developers.openai.com/plugins/quickstart.md, undated, fetched 2026-11-03]. The full plugin doc (488,087 characters, read in five 100,000-character windows) contains no sentence naming Free, Go, Plus or Pro as eligible or ineligible for custom servers; the only plan-related sentence is "Plugin extensions on the web are coming soon to ChatGPT Free and Go users", which concerns published plugin extensions, not adding a custom server [sourced: https://developers.openai.com/plugins/llms-full.txt, undated, fetched 2026-11-03; character count from the fetch tool]. Weight: strong against (b): a flow that requires a "Work" tab, `@`-invocation and a risk acknowledgement is a developer flow by construction, and the consumer-facing Chat tab is not mentioned as a place the plugin works.
2. **Every reachable secondary says the ChatGPT path is paid, web-only and (for Pro) read-only.** Customer.io, last updated 2026-10-28: "You must have a ChatGPT Pro, Plus, or Enterprise plan and enable Developer Mode in your ChatGPT account"; "You must be an admin in your ChatGPT account to enable Developer Mode"; "You can't complete this process on the mobile app" [sourced: https://docs.customer.io/ai/mcp/chatgpt/, 2026-10-28; secondary]. Zuplo: "Pro users can use read/fetch MCP servers only"; "Full MCP apps currently require ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, or Edu"; "Pro users can connect MCP servers with read/fetch permissions in Developer Mode ... but full MCP support is not currently available to Pro users" [sourced: https://zuplo.com/docs/mcp-gateway/connect-clients/chatgpt.md, undated, fetched 2026-11-03; secondary]. OpenAI's own article that both point to ("Developer mode and MCP apps in ChatGPT", help.openai.com 12584461) returned HTTP 403, as did "Connectors in ChatGPT" (11487775) and the release notes (6825453) [unknown: three help.openai.com fetches on 2026-11-03, all 403; the plan gate is therefore unconfirmed by a primary and the secondaries are the best available]. Weight: moderate. Two independent vendors who integrate with ChatGPT in production agree on paid + Developer Mode + web; neither is OpenAI.
3. **OpenAI's 2026 changelog has no consumer-plan opening.** The only 2026 MCP-in-ChatGPT entry is 2026-05-19, Secure MCP Tunnel, which "lets supported OpenAI products including ChatGPT web, Codex" reach private servers and was announced "for enterprise customers"; no 2026 entry mentions plugins, connectors, developer mode, Go, Free or mobile [sourced: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog, entries 2026-04-24, 2026-05-19, 2026-09-10, 2026-10-07, 2026-10-28, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: weak-to-moderate: absence in a developer changelog, but consistent with no consumer opening having happened.
4. **EU/EEA submission block.** "For now, projects with EU data residency cannot submit plugins with MCP servers for review"; "Use a project with global data residency" [sourced: developers.openai.com/plugins/llms-full.txt, fetched 2026-11-03]. The consumer-side EEA/UK/CH connector exclusion for Plus/Pro remains [unknown: help.openai.com 6825453 is 403; the snippet quoted in byo-ai.md is not treated as evidence]. Weight: weak for consumers directly, but it means a European startup cannot even list on ChatGPT without a non-EU project.

### Against (a)/(b) — Gemini's path is real but fenced

5. **Gemini custom apps: five gates and a desktop-only add step.** "Be 18 or over and in the US"; "Sign in to the Gemini app with a personal Google Account" and "For now, this feature isn't available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account"; Keep Activity on, "Custom apps are unavailable when this setting is off"; "Only available in English"; add happens in "the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com" on a computer; the server URL "must follow the standard MCP specifications"; no subscription tier stated; no limit on number of custom apps stated; warning text: "Google does not control, monitor, or secure third-party MCP servers"; "Custom apps may request data beyond what is strictly needed for your request" [sourced: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137?hl=en, undated (©2026 Google), fetched 2026-11-03]. Steps counted: 6 (Settings > Connected Apps > Custom apps > paste URL > optional manual credentials > continue and follow prompts) [estimated: count of the numbered steps on that page]. Weight: strong against "mainstream": Google's 900M monthly Gemini users are "across 230 countries and more than 70 languages" [sourced: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/, 2026-05-19, via byo-ai.md], and the custom-app path is US + English + 18+ + personal account + desktop add.
6. **Gemini's release notes never mention custom apps or MCP for consumers; the 2026 openings are for Google-curated apps.** 2026-10-13: "You can connect more than 40 apps or services to get things done in one conversation"; "Customize is rolling out globally across web, mobile, and the Gemini desktop app on Windows and macOS, for all user tiers"; but "Most connected apps are available in English in the U.S." 2026-05-19: new connections "starting with OpenTable, Canva, and Instacart". 2026-06-30: Spark "to Google AI Ultra subscribers aged 18 and over". 2026-01-20: Personal Intelligence beta "to Google AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers in the U.S." No 2026 entry mentions MCP or custom apps [sourced: https://gemini.google/release-notes/, entries 2026.01.20, 2026.05.19, 2026.06.30, 2026.09.10, 2026.10.13, fetched 2026-11-03 in two windows covering all 160,370 characters]. Weight: moderate. The curated catalogue is what Google promotes to consumers; the custom-URL path is a support-article feature.
7. **The developer side of Gemini is a gated preview.** Connected Apps Platform: "During the preview phase, we offer self-service onboarding to the Connected Apps Platform through Google Connected Console"; "Developers interested in joining can apply for preview" [sourced: https://developers.google.com/connected-apps, undated, fetched 2026-11-03]; the MCP guide: "You need to deploy your MCP server to a public HTTPS endpoint"; "Request access using the interest form"; "Test live tool execution using Gemini Developer Mode"; "submit your application for review and publication"; "Last updated 2026-10-27 UTC" [sourced: https://developers.google.com/connected-apps/mcp, 2026-10-27, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: moderate for the venture: the *listed* (discoverable, non-custom) path is behind an interest form and Google review with no published timeline; the custom-URL path is the only unreviewed one and it is the fenced one in (5).

### Against (b)/(c) — Even on Claude, the easiest surface, custom connectors are built and labelled as a power-user path

8. **Anthropic's own docs define the custom-connector path as the developer's test path.** "You test an MCP server against the real Claude client by adding it to Claude as a custom connector. There is no separate staging environment, so you test in production"; "Any Claude account can do this, on Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, or Enterprise" [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/testing.md, undated, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: moderate for (c): the feature the venture would ride is the one Anthropic tells developers to use for QA.
9. **A custom connector is second-class in the product.** Directory vs custom table: Anthropic review "Yes / No"; in-product discovery "Browse, search, Suggested Connectors / None"; "Appears as: Named card with logo / **Custom**"; external-link confirmation "Can allowlist destinations to skip the modal / Always shows the modal"; "Custom connectors are never suggested"; "Only a directory listing makes your connector discoverable inside Claude" [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/directory-vs-custom.md, undated, fetched 2026-11-03]. The add dialog carries a warning: "Custom connectors allow connections to unverified services. Claude can access and perform actions within these services" [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted.md, undated, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: moderate for (c). A wedge that shows up as a grey "Custom" card with an "unverified" warning, and that Claude will never recommend, is not shaped like a consumer wedge; the directory path (review, "Review time isn't fixed", Free accounts can't submit [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/directory/publish, via byo-ai.md Q2]) is the consumer-shaped one.
10. **Free is capped at one custom connector, and the no-OAuth shortcut is not generally available.** "On the Free plan, you can add one custom connector" [sourced: claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted.md, fetched 2026-11-03]. "Request header authentication is in beta and available to a limited set of organizations. If you don't see the Request headers section in the Add custom connector dialog, your organization doesn't have access yet" [sourced: same]. This narrows byo-ai.md's "no-auth or bearer allowed": bearer-header auth is a limited beta; a startup must ship OAuth (CIMD, DCR or a pre-registered client) or "No sign-in" (where "anyone with access to the server URL can use the connector"). Weight: weak for consumers, moderate for the startup's build cost.
11. **Adding happens on web/desktop; mobile is a use surface.** The getting-started page: "This page is for anyone who wants to add a connector to their own Claude account, in claude.ai or the Claude desktop app"; "A connected connector is available in your conversations on the web, desktop, and mobile"; "Claude mobile app: remote connectors" [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started.md, undated, fetched 2026-11-03]. Whether the **Add custom connector** dialog exists in the iOS/Android apps is [unknown: neither support.claude.com 11175166 nor the three claude.com/docs pages fetched say so; byo-ai.md's "Any client incl. mobile" for *setup* was an over-read of "connection originates from Anthropic's cloud across every Claude client", which is about where the connection runs, not where it is added]. Weight: moderate for (b): the photo use case is a phone use case; if the add step needs a desktop browser on all three assistants, the funnel starts off-device everywhere.
12. **Every step after adding is an approval step.** "Claude can ask for your approval before it uses one of the connector's tools. Select Allow once to continue, or Always allow to skip the prompt"; connectors must be toggled on per conversation via "+ > Connectors" [sourced: claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started.md, fetched 2026-11-03]; guidance says "Only click Always allow for trusted servers" [sourced: add-unlisted.md, fetched 2026-11-03]. ChatGPT: "ChatGPT currently requires manual confirmation in any conversation before write actions can be taken" [sourced: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/mcp/, via byo-ai.md Q1]. Weight: weak individually, but it is the texture of a power-user feature.

### Against (c) — No adoption evidence, one direct precedent failed, and the mechanism is a commodity

13. **No consumer-adoption number for custom connectors exists on any page fetched.** Anthropic publishes no consumer user count [unknown: byo-ai.md, re-confirmed by absence on all four claude.com/docs pages fetched today]; OpenAI's and Google's pages fetched today state no custom-server usage figures [unknown: no search budget to look further; nothing on developers.openai.com changelog, plugin docs, support.google.com 17209137 or gemini.google release notes]. Weight: this is the load-bearing absence. A wedge claim with zero usage evidence on any surface defaults to unproven.
14. **The one consumer company that sold "your corpus as a tool for any AI" shut its hosted service.** Khoj Cloud closed 2026-04-15 citing "challenges in defensibility with all the big labs coming out with similar products" and that "a subscription, cloud-first service, complex document syncs, custom data integrations, multiple (6!) clients" was "very difficult to scale in utility" [sourced: https://app.khoj.dev/, 2026-04-15, via byo-ai.md Q3]. Weight: moderate (text corpus, one case).
15. **The attach mechanism is a commodity, so even where it works it does not differentiate.** Official MCP registry 54,763 active servers on 2026-11-03 vs "10,000 active servers" on 2025-12-09 [sourced: registry.modelcontextprotocol.io API measured 2026-11-03; blog.modelcontextprotocol.io 2025-12-09; via byo-ai.md Q5]; 28 "immich mcp", 35 "google photos mcp", 22 "apple photos mcp" GitHub repos [sourced: GitHub search API, 2026-11-03, via byo-ai.md Q5]; PhotoPrism ships MCP in-product [sourced: docs.photoprism.app, via byo-ai.md Q6]. Weight: moderate for (c).

### Against (a) — Two consumer surfaces have no path

16. **Apple**: no MCP in any Apple primary through the June 2026 App Intents updates; native path is App Intents entities, where the photos-domain `search` and `openAsset` schemas are deprecated [sourced: developer.apple.com app-schema-domain-photos.json and updates/appintents.json, fetched 2026-11-03, via byo-ai.md Q1/Q4]. **Microsoft consumer Copilot**: four connector families, no custom connectors or MCP [sourced: support.microsoft.com, via byo-ai.md Q1]. Weight: strong for the share of consumers reached through the default phone assistant.

## Evidence FOR

1. **Claude: Free-tier, unreviewed, short, with a prefilled install link.** Add by URL "Works in: Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, and Enterprise plans"; Free/Pro/Max/Ultra steps: open Customize > Connectors, "Add custom connector", enter URL, optional OAuth credentials, "Add" — 5 numbered steps [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted.md, fetched 2026-11-03; step count [estimated: count of numbered steps on the page]]. A developer can link straight into the dialog: `https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=NAME&connectorUrl=ENCODED_URL`; "claude.ai opens the dialog with the name and URL prefilled"; "A signed-out user is prompted to sign in first and then sees the prefilled dialog" [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/directory-vs-custom.md, fetched 2026-11-03]. "a user or an organization Owner adds it by entering its URL, with no review by Anthropic" [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building, fetched 2026-11-03]. Once added, it is usable on "web, desktop, and mobile" [sourced: claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started.md, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: **strong for (a) and (b) on Claude.** From the startup's app: one tap on an install link → sign in → confirm → OAuth → done, then usable on the phone. That is close to the friction of connecting a third-party app to any OAuth service.
2. **Claude's OAuth client is consumer-grade.** Claude supports the 2025-03-26, 2025-06-18 and 2025-11-25 authorization specs, DCR, and a Client ID Metadata Document identity ("Use Claude's published identity: the recommended option ... You set nothing up"); lazy authentication lets "people use those [tools] right away and sign in only when Claude reaches a tool that needs it"; "Text and image-based tool results" are accepted; limits "~150,000 characters" and "240 seconds per tool call" [sourced: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building and custom/add-unlisted.md, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: moderate; the image-result path matters for photos, though inline rendering remains untested [unknown: no Claude account in this session].
3. **Gemini: a real custom-MCP path with no subscription stated, usable on mobile after a desktop add.** "Once connected, they're available on both the Gemini mobile app and Gemini web app"; DCR supported with manual client ID/secret fallback; no tier named [sourced: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137, fetched 2026-11-03]. Google's Customize/connected-apps surface is "rolling out globally across web, mobile, and the Gemini desktop app ... for all user tiers" as of 2026-10-13 [sourced: gemini.google/release-notes, entry 2026.10.13, fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: moderate for (a); the all-tiers rollout is for the curated catalogue, but it shows Google treating connected apps as a mass feature, and the custom path rides the same Connected Apps settings page.
4. **ChatGPT: the official path exists and OpenAI requires plugins to work on mobile.** "Plugins must function reliably in ChatGPT on both desktop and mobile, including any UI components"; "Plugin extensions on the web are coming soon to ChatGPT Free and Go users"; "Published plugins can run in ChatGPT and Codex" [sourced: developers.openai.com/plugins/llms-full.txt, fetched 2026-11-03]. No OpenAI page fetched states a plan gate for adding a custom server [sourced: developers.openai.com connect-chatgpt.md, quickstart.md, concepts/plugins.md, llms-full.txt, all fetched 2026-11-03]. Weight: weak-to-moderate. The absence of a stated gate on OpenAI's developer pages is not evidence of no gate (the secondaries say there is one and point at an OpenAI article this session cannot read), but the direction of travel ("coming soon to Free and Go") is toward more access, at least for published plugins.
5. **Direction of travel across the industry is toward opening.** MCP under the Linux Foundation's AAIF with Anthropic, OpenAI, Block, Google, Microsoft, AWS backing [sourced: https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2025-12-09-mcp-joins-agentic-ai-foundation/, 2025-12-09, via byo-ai.md]; Tier-1 SDK downloads "close to half-a-billion a month" [sourced: https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/, 2026-07-28, via byo-ai.md]; Google Connected Apps Platform preview 2026-10-28 with "more than 50 Connected Apps" [sourced: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/connected-apps-one-place/, 2026-10-28, via byo-ai.md]; Microsoft 365 Copilot federated connectors gallery at 178 data sources [estimated: hand count, via byo-ai.md, ms.date 2026-10-19]. Weight: moderate for (a) over a 12–24 month horizon; weak for (b)/(c) today.
6. **Claude Free can be the frictionless default and Gemini the second.** For a US adult: Claude (install link, Free) and Gemini (desktop add, no tier) both work today; that covers two of the three named assistants with primary-sourced flows [sourced: items 1 and 3 above]. Weight: this is what keeps the verdict at partial rather than broken.

## What observable fact would flip the verdict

To **holds** (any one of these, primary-sourced):
- An OpenAI page stating that Free/Go/Plus users can add a custom MCP server from the mobile app and use it in the ordinary Chat tab (not Work), without Developer Mode; or an OpenAI-published number of consumer accounts with a custom server attached.
- Google removing the US/English/desktop-add gates from support article 17209137, or stating a tier-free, mobile-add flow.
- Anthropic, OpenAI or Google publishing a consumer-adoption figure for custom/third-party connectors (for example, share of weekly active consumer accounts with any connector attached) in the tens of percent, or a consumer app whose public growth is attributable to a "connect to ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini" install link.
- Apple shipping an iOS consumer path (MCP or App Intents equivalent) that lets a third-party photo library answer Siri's "show me photos of X" with its own search, documented on developer.apple.com.

To **broken**:
- OpenAI documenting that custom MCP servers are Business/Enterprise/Edu-only (confirming the Zuplo reading) with no Plus/Pro/Free path.
- Google adding a paid-tier requirement to custom apps, or restricting them to Spark/AI Ultra.
- Anthropic removing custom connectors from the Free plan or moving them behind directory review.
- A security incident leading any of the three to require review for user-added servers.

## Implication for the venture

Treat BYO-AI as a *feature with one good door* (Claude install link; Gemini for US adults), not as the wedge. The realistic 2026 consumer funnel is: install the startup's app, tap "Connect to Claude", confirm, OAuth; everything else needs a desktop browser and, on ChatGPT, probably a paid plan and Developer Mode. The absence of any adoption figure for custom connectors on any assistant means the wedge claim cannot be supported today; the venture should instrument its own install-link conversion as the first number that could support it.
