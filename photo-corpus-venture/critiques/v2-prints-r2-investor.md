# Critique: v2-prints — round 2 — investor lens

Written 2026-11-03 by a fresh-context critic. Read in order: SCORECARD.md, variants/v2-prints.md, every file in research/ (including research/assumptions/A1–A6), variants/current.md. Did not read CHANGELOG, other v2 variants, or any critique.

## Stance

Cold seed investor. Default is no. I care about who pays, how much, why now, what the wedge is, what the distribution is, what must be true for a $1B+ outcome, and whether the founder is fooling themselves about platform risk. Allergic to "storage company" economics, to consumer products that need behaviour change, and to small channels dressed up as markets. Ceiling, Distribution and Platform risk scored hardest; every Ceiling input checked against research/sizing.md and the round-2 research files.

## Failed claims (variant asserts; research contradicts or does not support)

1. **"Legacybox ... ~200k/yr flat since 2019" (Customer) and "customer velocity is flat at ~200k/yr for six years" (Why now #8), used as G = 200k/yr in the Ceiling.** research/digitization-market.md Q2 and Q9 item 2: the "flat" conclusion is withdrawn; the 2M-families claim is undated; only a ≈190k/yr 2019–21 velocity and a ≈180k/yr floor exist; trend [unknown].
2. **"prints made per year fell to 42B in 2013" (Why now #8).** research/digitization-market.md Q9 item 3 and numbers table: withdrawn — source 403 on every attempt, attribution unverified.
3. **Ancestry/iMemories "largest acquisition in terms of revenue" (Why now #1).** research/digitization-market.md Q3: the Fast Company quote is "unverified and not used as evidence below".
4. **"The consent-aware, on-device design is now a litigated defence, not a guess" (Why now #5).** research/unknowns-r1.md Q8: Doe v. Apple (2026-09-30) rejected G.T. as "the silver bullet", kept the iCloud Faceprint Subclass alive on a theory that reaches encrypted sync, and held that control does not require readability. The variant acknowledges this only as a residual.
5. **Day-1 "files already sitting in their normal photo library with the names attached."** research/ios-feasibility.md Q9: neither Apple Photos nor Google Photos rebuilds People from XMP; Apple imports keywords only on the Mac path. On iOS the names are inside the file, not in the user's Photos UI. The variant's later "portable names, not portable People albums" is the honest version; the Day-1 sentence is not.
6. **Pool "61.5M households headed by 55+" as "the evidence-anchored core of the 'box of prints' pool."** research/sizing.md row 40 says no survey measures print ownership; the figure is a demographic count from a new web fact, not a count of households with boxes.
7. **Ceiling price $60–70 blended.** The one comparable's realised ARPU is ≈$48/yr (research/digitization-market.md Q1), which the variant itself cites in Customer and then does not carry into the Ceiling; at $48 the $100M target is 2.08M payers, 5.2× Photomyne.

## Most damaging objection

Nobody has been shown to pay for the layer you are charging for. The only audited P&L in this category (Photomyne, 402k payers, 599M scans) shows customers tag 0.10 times per scan and pay $48/yr for *backup*; the two genealogy incumbents with ~4.8M paying subscribers already give away or bundle the dating and deceased-face layer (MyHeritage PhotoDater free, Photo plan $49.90/yr with scanning; Ancestry Photo Insights, Face Match, and now the mail-in box itself at $0.49/photo). Your $60/yr revenue line therefore rests on lifting a demonstrated 0.10 tag rate five-fold with a feature set two incumbents have partly shipped — while your own acquisition math (15–23-month payback on paid UA against a 15.6-month category life; the comparable spends 64–68% of revenue on Facebook and loses money) says you cannot buy the users to test it. The variant's honest ceiling ($25–45M US) is the answer to "what must be true for $1B": nothing in the evidence.

## Weakest dimension

**Ceiling (2).** Built on a withdrawn velocity figure (Legacybox "flat 200k/yr") as the G anchor, a withdrawn print-volume figure, a demographic proxy for a pool the sizing file marks [unknown], a price above the comparable's ARPU, and a retention regime (91%) the economics file reserves for systems of record — which an index-only product is not. The variant's own arithmetic lands $100M in year ~15.

## Three questions for the founder

1. Photomyne's 402,000 payers name 0.10 photos per scan and pay for backup. What have you seen — pre-sales, deposits, interviews with a named n — that says a scanner will pay $60/yr for naming, dating and backs when the incumbent cannot get them to name for free?
2. Ancestry now owns the box (iMemories at $0.49/photo), the tree, Face Match, and Photo Insights; MyHeritage ships PhotoDater free and sells a $49.90/yr photo plan with scanning. If either turns on living-face naming with subject consent — the one thing you say they avoid — what is the business?
3. Your paid-UA payback is 15–23 months against a 15.6-month category life, and the comparable spends two-thirds of revenue on Facebook and is loss-making. Name the one non-paid channel you would bet the seed round on, and the number you have for it today.
