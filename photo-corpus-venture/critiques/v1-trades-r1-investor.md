# Critique: v1-trades — round 1 — cold seed investor

Written 2026-11-03. Fresh-context critic; read SCORECARD.md, variants/v1-trades.md, every file in research/ and research/assumptions/. Did not read CHANGELOG, other variants, or other critiques.

## Stance

Cold seed investor. Default is no. I care about who pays, how much, why now, the wedge, the distribution, what must be true for a $1B+ outcome, and whether the founder is fooling themselves about platform risk. I am allergic to storage-company economics, to consumer products that need behaviour change, and to small channels dressed up as markets. I score Ceiling, Distribution and Platform risk hardest.

One-paragraph verdict: this is the first variant in this project that does not need a migration or a new capture habit, and its economics are the opposite of a storage company — credit where due. It is also a product whose own author computes a $17–34M ARR ceiling, whose incumbent already ships GPS-matched camera-roll import and an MCP server, and whose target population has produced zero dollars of spend on this job in 156 Reddit posts and 264 comments. That is a nice small business, not a seed-stage venture, unless one of the founder's own "(b)–(d)" escape hatches is shown to be real.

## Most damaging objection

You price Solo at $12/mo because the 1–9-employee long tail will not pay CompanyCam's $63–79/mo, and your own ceiling math says that at that price the US business tops out at $17–34M ARR; the only on-page path to $100M is ARPU ≥ $600/yr from that same long tail. Both cannot be true. Meanwhile the one incumbent you are routing around already ships GPS-matched camera-roll import by project address (Magic Uploader), text scanning, and an MCP server, has $415M of fresh capital, has no free tier yet, and your document states the switching cost against it "is zero on day 1". So the question that ends the conversation: **name the dated fact that shows a 1–9-employee contractor paying more than $0 today for retrieval or evidence (not capture, not PM), and the mechanism by which that spend reaches $600/yr before CompanyCam ships "index my camera roll into projects" as a free top-of-funnel.** The research has 156 posts and 264 comments from this population and the only software price anyone named was a vendor's pitch.

## Weakest dimension

**Ceiling (2).** The founder has pre-conceded it: "Without (b)–(d) this tops out as a $20–40M ARR company." For a seed investor who needs a believable $100M ARR path, that sentence is the whole memo. Distribution (3) and Platform risk (3) are the two that would decide whether even the $20–40M is reachable; both rest on `[unknown]` channels and a "they chose not to" defence.

## Unknowns to research before the next round

1. What share of US construction establishments with 0–9 employees use a dedicated job-photo app (CompanyCam, Jobber, Housecall Pro photo features, ServiceTitan) versus phone camera roll and chat only? (JBKnowledge ConTech report; any 2024–2026 contractor technology survey; the variant's load-bearing assumption 1.)
2. How many customer disputes, warranty denials, GC back-charges or insurance claims per year does a 1–9-employee residential plumbing/HVAC/electrical/roofing contractor experience, and in what share of them is photo evidence decisive? (Levelset post-2019 data, Federated Insurance / NEXT / Hartford claims guidance, state contractor-board complaint statistics.)
3. Has CompanyCam, Jobber or Housecall Pro shipped or announced (release notes, help center, community roadmap, app-store changelogs 2025–2026) automatic camera-roll-to-project import, whole-roll indexing, natural-language photo search, or a free tier; and what is CompanyCam's current paying-company count?
4. What is the customer acquisition cost and trial-to-paid conversion for a $100–500/yr field-service or trades SaaS product sold to 1–9-employee contractors? (ServiceTitan 10-K sales-and-marketing per net new customer; Jobber/Housecall Pro disclosures; any trades-vertical app-store benchmark for CPI and conversion.)
5. Do manufacturer-warranty desks, home-warranty companies, insurers' contractor-claims processes, or small-claims courts treat a hash-attested, EXIF-tabulated photo packet differently from a bare photo attachment — any documented requirement, acceptance policy, or case outcome? (The variant's assumption 7; FRE 902(14) does not reach small claims.)

## Three questions for the founder

1. **Price versus ceiling.** Solo is $144/yr because the target will not pay CompanyCam's $756/yr. Your $100M path (b) needs $600/yr from the same customer. Which number is wrong, and what evidence do you have for the other one?
2. **The incumbent's free tier.** CompanyCam's Magic Uploader already pulls camera-roll photos by project address, and it already exposes the account to Claude and ChatGPT. If CompanyCam ships "index your whole camera roll into auto-created projects" as a free plan the quarter after you show traction, what do your customers have on day 90 that they would lose by switching? Your document says the switching cost is zero on day 1 and needs "roughly 18 months of head start" — what is the 18-month plan, in order, with dates?
3. **The ten libraries.** Before you ask for money, run the week-4 test on ten real contractor camera rolls recruited from your own network: what was job-level clustering precision and recall, what fraction of job photos had no usable GPS (indoor, dense street, texted by crew), how long did the index take on an iPhone 13-class device that lacks Apple Intelligence, and did Apple App Review accept full-library PhotoKit access for an indexing app under guideline 5.1.1(iii)? Bring the numbers, not the plan.
