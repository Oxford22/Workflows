# v1-trades — Round 1 critique — BUILDER

Date: 2026-11-03. Fresh-context critic; read SCORECARD.md, variants/v1-trades.md, and every file in research/ (including research/assumptions/A1–A6). Did not read CHANGELOG, other variants, or other critiques.

**Stance.** Builder. I believe only shipped demos. I care about the smallest thing that proves the core loop, the ugliest technical risk, and what breaks at 10k users (cost, support burden, data-loss liability, legal exposure). Buildability and Economics are scored hardest. Founder fit is [unknown] and scored 5 by rule.

**One-paragraph read.** v1-trades is the first variant in this project whose core loop can be put on a phone in four weeks without a migration step, and the author tagged almost every number. That is why it beats v0. But the 4-week spec as written contains three items that are platform-gated on iOS 27 or unmeasured, and the paid artifact (the hash-attested evidence packet) rests on a design contradiction: the index runs on 1536×2048 optimized-storage derivatives, so the SHA-256 "recorded at index time" is not a hash of the original unless every original is downloaded from iCloud, which is exactly the friction the variant was designed to avoid. Several pricing and statute numbers in the variant are the stale or incomplete versions that research/trades-and-reddit.md explicitly corrected.

## What breaks at 10k users

- **Cost.** Index-only COGS is sound; at 10k Solo users the variable infra is ~$3–7k/mo. Not the problem.
- **Support burden.** $0.25/user/mo = $2,500/mo across 10k contractors, i.e. a quarter of one support hire, for a product whose failure mode ("the Nguyen job has the neighbour's photos in it"; "Index stopped again"; "my apprentice's phone won't join") is a phone call from a tradesperson, not a ticket. Jobber and Housecall Pro charge $29–79/mo and staff phone support. No research file benchmarks B2B SMB support cost; the $0.25 is flagged by the author and I treat it as unsupported. [research/unit-economics.md Q7 convention; no benchmark found]
- **Data-loss liability.** The sold promise is "the one thing the camera roll and a phone upgrade will not do for them": a 15-year packet archive. On single-copy, single-region B2. A5.md Against #2 says a single-copy store "does not credibly deliver" permanence and the privacy comparable (Ente) runs three copies on three providers. More importantly, the archive is only as durable as the company: if a $20–40M-ceiling company folds in year 4, 10k contractors lose the evidence they were told would outlast their phone. That is a reputational and arguably contractual exposure that no COGS line captures.
- **Legal exposure, wrong-house packets.** The variant says it: "an evidence packet that includes the wrong house's photos is worse than no packet." At 10k users and 10 packets/mo quota, even 1% cluster contamination puts ~1,000 contaminated packets a month into deposit fights and warranty desks. Precision is [unknown].
- **Legal exposure, crew phones.** Crew apps index the crew member's *personal* camera roll and push "metadata + 512 px thumbnails + embeddings" of job clusters to the company index. A misclassified cluster (the apprentice's own house is 60 m from a job; GPS is 50–200 m off) pushes an employee's personal photos to the boss's server. No faces means no BIPA, correct; but this is an employee-privacy BYOD problem with no consent architecture in the spec. [face-regulation.md Q7 architecture item 2 is about subjects; nothing in research covers employee camera-roll indexing]
- **App Review.** Guideline 5.1.1(iii) says to prefer the picker over full Photos access; a job-photo indexer has a core-functionality claim, but rejection probability is [unknown] in A2.md and nothing in this variant addresses it.

## Most damaging objection

**"Your evidence packet's SHA-256 is computed over which bytes?"** With Optimize iPhone Storage on, the index runs on 1536×2048 derivative copies and the originals live in iCloud [ios-feasibility.md Q4; platform-access.md Q1]. So either (a) the app downloads every original on first index to hash it — 30k per-request iCloud fetches at an undocumented, field-reported 3–4-concurrent rate, i.e. the hours-on-Wi-Fi friction this variant exists to avoid [A2.md Against #4] — or (b) the hash is first computed at packet-export time, in which case "we recorded this hash in March 2027" says nothing about March 2024 capture that the EXIF the owner can already mail from Apple Photos does not say, and the "attested timeline" compounding asset is empty. If the founder cannot say which, and show that (b)-plus-EXIF changes an adjuster's or small-claims judge's decision versus "Select → Share → Unmodified Originals," the product's paid artifact is a PDF generator and the retrieval layer is competing with Apple Photos Places and Collections for $144/yr.

## Weakest dimension

**Distribution (3)**, tied with Ceiling (3). Ceiling is honest and structural; the variant already concedes $17–34M. Distribution is the one a founder would have to fix to make even that number: there is no channel with evidence of working, the association channel reaches the wrong slice (larger shops, 5.7%), Reddit is hostile, supply houses and insurers are [unknown], and the only CAC figure is a consumer iOS CPI spliced across three populations and applied to a B2B SMB buyer.

## Unknowns to research before round 2

1. Does `com.apple.developer.background-tasks.continued-processing.inference` exist in the shipping iOS 27.x SDK, how is it requested, and does a Core ML MobileCLIP model on the Neural Engine keep executing inside a BGContinuedProcessingTask after the app is backgrounded or the phone is locked on iOS 27? (One device test with Xcode 27 answers this.)
2. Is "Optimize iPhone Storage" the default on a new iPhone, and in a 20–50k-asset library what share of assets return `PHImageResultIsInCloudKey == true` with network access disallowed — i.e. how many originals must be downloaded to SHA-256 them, and at what sustained concurrency does iCloud serve `isNetworkAccessAllowed` requests?
3. On iPhone camera-roll photos taken indoors at residential job sites, what is the GPS presence rate and horizontal accuracy, and does iOS Messages (and WhatsApp) preserve location EXIF on received photos saved to Photos on iOS 26/27?
4. Do the Apple ML Research Model Terms of Use ("apple-amlr") for MobileCLIP / MobileCLIP2 weights permit commercial redistribution inside a paid App Store app and in an Android LiteRT port, or must the index use an Apache/MIT model (SigLIP 2 B/16) with a different on-device latency?
5. What is the measured CAC, trial-to-paid, and first-year retention for a self-serve $100–$250/yr trades SaaS app (Jobber, Housecall Pro, CompanyCam Core, or a Jobber App Marketplace tool), from any filing, investor deck, or reviews, versus the consumer Photo & Video $65–90 derivation?

## Three questions for the founder

1. With Optimize iPhone Storage on, which bytes does the packet's SHA-256 cover, when is it first computed, and what does the adjuster or judge get from your packet that they do not get from thirty unmodified originals mailed out of Apple Photos with their EXIF?
2. On an iPhone 17 running iOS 27, the owner taps Index on a 30,000-photo roll and then opens the Camera to shoot the next job. What happens to the run, how many "tap Index" sessions does the full roll take in practice, and does MobileCLIP keep running on the Neural Engine once the phone is locked without the Background Inference entitlement?
3. Of your first ten pilot contractors, how many have an all-iPhone crew, and what exactly does Crew ($39/mo) sell to a 3-person shop where two techs are on Android during the 6+ weeks before the v1.1 indexing client, given that Play's READ_MEDIA_IMAGES declaration review has not been attempted?
