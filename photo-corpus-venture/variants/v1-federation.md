# v1-federation — Family Index (no migration)

Written 2026-11-03 for round 1. Attacks the round-0 weakest dimension (Wedge 2/10): nothing is migrated. Each relative's library stays where it is; only an index and small previews are pooled. Tags per README. Where a research file was silent, the claim is marked [unknown] with what would resolve it. WebSearch budget was exhausted before this variant was written (4 queries refused), so new evidence this pass is four direct WebFetches (Apple 108047, Google One 9004014, Queryable README, Apple PhotoKit index); everything else cites research/.

## One line

One natural-language search across everyone's phones in the family — iPhone, Mac, later Android — built from indexes computed on each relative's own device over their existing iCloud/Google library, with a user-confirmed shared People graph and a bring-your-own-AI endpoint; originals never move.

## Customer (who; how many [tagged]; what they do today; what they pay today)

- **Who.** Families whose photos sit in two or more accounts and often two platforms: adult children + parents + siblings. First user is the family's "memory keeper" (the person who already fields "do you have the photo of…" requests). Second users are relatives who install because the keeper asked.
- **How many (US).** Multi-person households 94.3M [estimated: 132.8M households − 38.5M one-person; research/sizing.md §3 rows 9–10, Census 2024-11-12]. Households with ≥2 smartphone users, i.e. ≥2 photo accounts: ~78M [estimated: 94.3M × 0.91²; sizing.md row 20]. Mixed iPhone/Android households: 28–47M [estimated: 94.3M × 30–50%; no survey exists — sizing.md row 19]. Multigenerational households ~19M [estimated: 22% of ~340M people ÷ 4; Pew 2026-03-25 via sizing.md row 21]. Cross-platform households that already pay for a photo cloud: 15–30M [estimated: sizing.md worked chain (a)]. Device gate for the on-device assistant layer: Apple-Intelligence-capable iPhones are 69.6% of TelemetryDeck's top-10 models and plausibly 50–60% of the active base [estimated: research/ios-feasibility.md Q5, TelemetryDeck CSV 2026-10-26]; the CLIP/face index itself runs on any iPhone on iOS 18+ (Queryable ships MobileCLIP on-device for iOS 18+) [sourced: https://github.com/mazzzystar/Queryable README, fetched 2026-11-03].
- **What they do today.** Shared Albums (cap 5,000 items, not backed up) and iCloud Shared Photo Library (creator + 5, one per person, moves items rather than merging; no People sharing documented) [sourced: support.apple.com/108314 and HT213248 via research/customers.md Q1(c), research/competitive.md Q1]; Google Partner Sharing (one partner; "Face labels are private to each account and aren't shared across accounts") [sourced: support.google.com/photos/answer/7378858 and /6128838, fetched 2026-11-03 via competitive.md Q2]; texting photos; asking the relative who "has it". Merge threads recur unresolved on Apple Community 2019/2021/2022/2025 and HN 2022–2024 [sourced: customers.md Q4 table].
- **What they pay today.**
  - Storage: iCloud+ 200 GB $2.99/mo, "Share everything with up to five other family members" [sourced: https://support.apple.com/en-us/108047, published 2026-09-16, fetched 2026-11-03]; Google One 100 GB $1.99, AI Plus 400 GB $4.99 [sourced-secondary: aggregators via research/unit-economics.md Q5; official page renders no prices], shareable with a family group [sourced: https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9004014, fetched 2026-11-03; no maximum stated].
  - The merge itself: ~$40 one-time (PowerPhotos) is the only money found changing hands [sourced: research/assumptions/A3.md sub-claim iii].
  - Products priced on the exact job publish no customers: Mylio Family $480–$732/yr with 5 h of human help; KinPhotos $49/yr [sourced: A3.md Against #1–#2].
  - Adjacent spend that does move: scanning $0.49–$1.28/print, organizers $50–$150/hr, family-capture apps $36–$132/yr [sourced: customers.md Q2 table].

## Wedge (first use case adopted without changing habits; the first 10 minutes; day-1 value for ONE user; day-7 value)

**First use case.** "Find any family photo from your own phone, without anyone moving anything." Install → grant full Photos access (the same prompt every backup app shows) → tap "Index". Nothing is uploaded except a ~256 px preview and vectors; nothing is cancelled, exported or re-homed. No Takeout, no osxphotos, no nights plugged in.

**The first 10 minutes (iOS 27, one user).**
1. Grant Photos access (`.authorized`); the app enumerates the library and requests thumbnails with network access off, skipping assets where `PHImageResultIsInCloudKey` is true [sourced: Apple PhotoKit docs via research/ios-feasibility.md Q3–Q4]. Under Optimize Storage the local copy is ~1536×2048 for most assets [sourced-secondary: budgethomelab.com 2026-06-06 via ios-feasibility.md Q4]; the share of assets with no local copy at all is [unknown].
2. Indexing runs as a user-initiated `BGContinuedProcessingTask` with a Live Activity (continues when the user switches apps; cancellable; no published max runtime) [sourced: Apple docs via ios-feasibility.md Q2]. MobileCLIP-S2 image embeddings (3.6 ms/image on iPhone 12 Pro Max) + Vision face detection + face embedding (<4 ms/face per Apple 2021): ~15 min for 30,000 photos, ~22 min derated — no OCR, no captions in the first pass [estimated: ios-feasibility.md Q3 Scenario A inputs]. A 30k-photo library is therefore searchable within the first session; OCR and captions run later, selectively.
3. Search box returns results at minute ~15. Named people start as anonymous clusters ("Person 7").

**Day-1 value for ONE user, stated against Siri AI / Ask Photos (honest).** For a US user on an iPhone 15 Pro/16+ running iOS 27, natural-language search is already native (iOS 18.1 since 2024-10-28; Siri AI since 2026-09-14) [sourced: Apple newsroom via research/ai-why-now.md Q7, competitive.md Q1]. What one user gets that Siri AI does not:
- (a) natural-language search on iPhones that are not Apple-Intelligence-capable — Apple's search needs iPhone 15 Pro / 16+ [sourced: Apple newsroom 2024-10-28 via ai-why-now.md Q7]; non-capable models (13, 14, 15) are 30.4% of TelemetryDeck's top-10 [sourced: TelemetryDeck CSV 2026-10-26 via ios-feasibility.md Q5] — but Queryable already gives this for free [sourced: github.com/mazzzystar/Queryable, ~3.0k stars];
- (b) the same index on a Mac, and the same query answered by Claude or Gemini instead of Apple's model via the Foundation Models `LanguageModel` protocol (beta) [sourced: github.com/anthropics/ClaudeForFoundationModels; firebase.blog 2026-06-09 via ios-feasibility.md Q1];
- (c) a working search in the EU, where Siri AI is absent on iOS at launch [sourced: Apple newsroom 2026-09-14 via competitive.md Q1];
- (d) names you assign are yours — exported as XMP `PersonInImage` + MWG regions (Apple exposes no People API on iOS at all) [sourced: ios-feasibility.md Q9; platform-access.md Q1].
Verdict: day-1 single-user value is modest and partly commoditised; this is why the single-user tier is free. It exists to make day 7 possible without a migration.

**Day-7 value: the second relative joins.** The keeper sends an invite; the relative installs and indexes on their own phone (same 10 minutes). The family now has (i) one search across both libraries ("Grandma at the lake, any of us, any year") with results drawn from both accounts and both platforms; (ii) assisted People merging: the keeper's "Mom" cluster and the sibling's "Person 3" are matched on the keeper's device from exchanged face vectors and presented as a *suggestion* to confirm — never auto-merged across decades (child→adult across decades is an open research problem; adult↔aged adult is ~solved on AgeDB-30 at 98%) [sourced: research/ai-why-now.md Q3]; (iii) cross-library duplicate pairs ("you both have this one"). None of this is shipped by Apple (Shared Library: no People sharing documented; a 2022 community report says People do not sync; 2026 behaviour [unknown]) [sourced: ios-feasibility.md Q6], Google (face labels private per account) [sourced: support.google.com/photos/answer/6128838], or Amazon (Family Vault pools uploads into one Prime store, six members, add-to-vault not merge; whether People clusters are shared is [unknown]) [sourced: research/sizing.md Q6]. The nearest shipped equivalent is Immich v3.3.0's cross-user people sharing (2026-10-07), free, self-hosted, same-server only [sourced: github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v3.3.0 via A3.md Against #3].

**Why the incumbents' sharing features are not this.**
- They all *move or copy media into one account's store*: Shared Library items "move out of your Personal Library and into the Shared Library" [sourced: support.apple.com/HT213248 via A4.md For #1]; Family Vault uploads draw on the Prime owner's allowance [sourced-secondary: sizing.md Q6]; Shared Albums are copies capped at 5,000 items [sourced: support.apple.com/108314 via customers.md].
- None crosses the Apple/Google boundary, and none carries identity across accounts.
- This variant pools *indexes*, the only object that can be pooled without a migration or a storage owner.

## Why now (dated changes since 2024, each tagged)

1. **iOS 27 (shipped 2026-09-14): Foundation Models gained image input on-device and a `LanguageModel` protocol with Claude and Gemini as listed providers, with tool calling** — an app with PhotoKit permission can run a third-party model over the user's existing library, no export [sourced: WWDC26 session 241; developer.apple.com/foundation-models-framework; ClaudeForFoundationModels README v0.1.0; firebase.blog 2026-06-09 — all via ios-feasibility.md Q1 and assumptions/A4.md Against #5]. PCC server model is free to developers under 2M downloads (daily per-user cap unpublished) [sourced: developer.apple.com/private-cloud-compute/ via ios-feasibility.md Q1].
2. **iOS 26 (2025-09-15): `BGContinuedProcessingTask`** — a sanctioned, user-initiated long-running task with a Live Activity, which is what makes a one-session index of a 30k library possible without keeping the screen on [sourced: Apple docs via ios-feasibility.md Q2]. Caveat: iOS 27 added a Background Inference entitlement that *restricts* ANE use in the background; its request process is undocumented [sourced: ios-feasibility.md Q2].
3. **MobileCLIP2 (2025-08-28)** and SigLIP 2 (2025-02): phone-class image encoders at 3–15 ms, MIT code [sourced: arxiv.org/abs/2508.20691; github.com/apple/ml-mobileclip via ios-feasibility.md Q3]. Capability existed pre-2024 (Queryable, Ente); what is new is Apple's own models and the OS task to run them.
4. **MCP went from nonexistent (2024-11-25) to standard across Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini (2026)**, with Claude custom connectors on the Free plan via a prefilled install link [sourced: claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted.md via assumptions/A6.md For #1; ai-why-now.md Q6].
5. **Android: ML Kit GenAI Embeddings API (alpha, 2026-10-26)**, EmbeddingGemma 2 text+image on AICore devices [sourced: developers.google.com/ml-kit/release-notes via ios-feasibility.md Q7]; foreground-only rule conflicts with the embeddings page [unknown which applies].
6. **G.T. v. Samsung (7th Cir., Aug 2026)**: face templates that stay on-device and that the company cannot access are outside BIPA's "possess/collect" — a dated legal reason to build the index on-device with company-unreadable sync [sourced: Mayer Brown 2026-08-20 via research/face-regulation.md Q1].
7. **Demand signals dated 2026**: Immich shipped cross-user people sharing after a 2.5-year request thread (2026-10-07) [sourced: A3.md]; Apple opened Shared Albums to Android/Windows contributors at full resolution (2026-06-08) [sourced: macrumors.com 2026-06-08 via customers.md Q1(c)]; Google's 2026-10-16 policy lets connected Photos data personalise, serve ads and train models if opted in — a trust contrast for an on-device indexer [sourced: blog.google update 2026-10-16 via ai-why-now.md Q5].
Each of 1–5 helps Apple/Google at least as much as a startup (ai-why-now.md §6); the combination that does not — cross-account, cross-platform identity with no storage owner — is the only part Apple and Google have not shipped (A4 clause (a) holds, re-verified 2026-11-03).

## Product (core loop; explicitly NOT in v1)

**Core loop.**
1. Index on device: thumbnails from local copies → MobileCLIP image vectors, face detect + embed, EXIF/time/place; OCR and short captions later, only on flagged assets (OCR on every image is ~2.5–3.7 h for 30k) [estimated: ios-feasibility.md Q3 Scenario B].
2. Sync to the family space: per-asset vector (~2.3 KB), face vectors, metadata and a ~50 KB preview; encrypted with a family key the company does not hold (Ente pattern) [sourced: research/face-regulation.md Q6–Q7].
3. Search: text → MobileCLIP text vector → nearest neighbours across all members' vectors; results show whose library each hit lives in and deep-link to the owner's Photos.
4. People: clusters per member; cross-member match suggestions computed on the keeper's device; a cluster becomes a named person only when confirmed, and a living non-member is named only after they accept an invite and a separate biometric-consent screen (or are marked deceased) [sourced: face-regulation.md Q7 architecture].
5. Assistant: on-device Foundation Model session with a `search_index` tool; optional PCC; optional Claude/Gemini through the same session API; and a remote MCP server over the family index (OAuth) with a Claude install link [sourced: A6.md For #1].
6. Annotations: voice/text notes, dates, places on any hit, stored in the family space and exportable.

**Explicitly NOT in v1.**
- Storing originals (no system-of-record claim; see Economics).
- Automatic cross-generation face matching (assisted only).
- Migration tools (no Takeout, no osxphotos, no iOS upload extension).
- Android assistant layer (index yes, later; no OS `LanguageModel` equivalent) [sourced: ios-feasibility.md Q7].
- Naming living people who have not consented.
- Server-side face matching for IL/TX/WA/EU accounts [sourced: face-regulation.md Q7 item 5].

## Business model and pricing (with comparables, tagged)

- **Free: single user, own index, on-device search, XMP export.** Zero marginal server cost for search (on-device) and a few cents of storage (previews + vectors ~1.2 GB on R2 ≈ $0.02/mo) [estimated: unit-economics.md Q7 index-only row; 50 KB preview assumption].
- **Family plan: $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr, up to 6 members** (matches Apple's creator + 5 and Amazon's 6). Annual-first in the app and on the web, because the retention regime differs by term: RevenueCat Photo & Video first annual renewal 23% (life ~15.6 months) vs first monthly renewal 48% (~1.9 months) [sourced: revenuecat.com 2026 via unit-economics.md Q6].
- **Comparables.** iCloud+ 200 GB $2.99/mo shared with five [sourced: support.apple.com/108047, 2026-09-16]; Google One 100 GB $1.99, AI Plus 400 GB $4.99 [sourced-secondary: via unit-economics.md Q5]; FamilyAlbum Premium Family $5.99/mo or $59/yr, Family Pro $10.99/mo or $109/yr [sourced: family-album.com/en/premium via customers.md Q1(h)]; Tinybeans+ Family $119.99/yr (6 accounts, 1 TB), Tinybeans+ $74.99/yr, ARPU $75 [sourced: ASX filings via customers.md Q1(h)]; Mylio Family $480/yr [sourced: A3.md]. $39.99/yr sits below the family-capture apps and above a single iCloud+ tier; it is a *second bill* on top of storage the family keeps paying, which is the pricing risk (customer critic, round 0).
- **Add-ons (not modelled in the base case).** Third-party model usage via Foundation Models bills the developer's workspace under App Attest [sourced: ClaudeForFoundationModels README via ios-feasibility.md Q1]; v1 ships the on-device model and PCC free, and routes Claude/Gemini either through the user's own assistant (MCP, user's plan pays) or a metered add-on priced later. Rate limits for Claude via App Attest [unknown].

## Distribution (non-paid channels with evidence; one paid channel with CAC math if any)

1. **The invite is the product.** No family plan exists until a second relative installs, so every paying unit carries ≥1 built-in invite. Evidence the mechanic works in this category: FamilyAlbum reached ~30M registered users on an invite-only family album [sourced: Mixi PDF 2026-05-07 via competitive.md Q5] — but on a newborn wedge and free unlimited storage subsidised by a listed parent, which this variant does not have. Honest: no family-archive (non-newborn) app has published invite-driven growth [unknown]. Falsifier: measured invite→install conversion below ~20% in the first 500 families.
2. **Claude/Gemini connector install link.** A user who asks Claude about "my family photos" can be sent to a prefilled custom-connector dialog; Claude Free allows one custom connector [sourced: claude.com/docs via A6.md]. Reach is bounded by Claude's consumer base (count unpublished [unknown]); Gemini custom apps need US/18+/English/desktop add [sourced: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137]; ChatGPT consumer eligibility [unknown]. Treat as a feature, not a channel (byo-ai.md §3).
3. **Professional photo organizers.** 700+ members, 2–3 new clients/month each, clients "scattered everywhere" across devices and clouds; a sanctioned $1,500 live member focus group and $750–$2,000 exhibitor slots at the April 2027 conference [sourced: research/pro-channel.md Q1–Q2, Q7]. A no-migration tool fits their fixed-fee operators (4 of 7 pricing pages found are fixed-fee or per-source) [estimated: pro-channel.md Q7]. Size of the whole channel's labour pool is $17–50M/yr [estimated: A3.md Against #7] — a credibility and referral channel, not volume.
4. **One paid channel, with math.** Apple Ads, Photo & Video CPI $2.79 (US, 2025) [sourced: apptweak.com 2026-08-19 via sizing.md Q4]; install→trial 14.0%, trial→paid 30.5% (Adapty) → ~$65 per payer, ~$90 at RevenueCat's 22.2% [estimated: sizing.md row 33]. Against a family-plan contribution of ~$2.62/mo on annual terms (Economics) and a 15.6-month expected life, max CAC ≈ $41 [estimated: $2.62 × 15.6]. Paid UA is underwater unless each paid install yields ≥1.6 additional organic paying families through invites [estimated: $65 ÷ $41]. Qeepsake's disclosed $35 CAC [sourced: Tinybeans Q3 FY26 via customers.md Q1(h)] is the only in-category number that clears; it is treated as the target, not the plan.

## Economics (per-customer COGS, GM, payback; cite research/unit-economics.md rows or derive with tags)

Reference family = 3 indexed members [estimated: assumption; no data on members per family plan], each a 100 GB / 22,000-image reference user (unit-economics.md Q2).
- **Per member per month.** Previews + vectors on R2: $0.02 [estimated: unit-economics.md Q7 index-only row]; vector DB share: $0.05 (disk-resident pgvector, $48 DigitalOcean box ≈ 1,000 users) [estimated: unit-economics.md Q4]; embedding compute: ~$0 (on device) vs $0.04–$0.11 one-time if done server-side [estimated: unit-economics.md Q3(a)]; selective server captions, if used: $0.69 one-time per 22k images on Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite batch [estimated: unit-economics.md Q3(b)] — excluded from v1 base. Hosted LLM queries: $0 (on-device / PCC free under 2M downloads / user's own assistant) [sourced: WWDC26 319 via ios-feasibility.md Q1]; $0.04/mo per querying member if we host [estimated: unit-economics.md Q4].
- **Per family per month: COGS ≈ 3 × $0.07 = $0.21** [estimated]. At $39.99/yr via App Store Small Business 15%: gross $3.33/mo, net $2.83, contribution **$2.62, GM 79% of gross** [estimated: Apple SBP 15% sourced via unit-economics.md Q7]. At $4.99/mo: net $4.24, contribution $4.03, **GM 81%**. Direct web billing via Stripe (2.9% + $0.30) lifts both ~10 points; this matches the 86% index-only figure in unit-economics.md Q7 within the fee-channel difference.
- **Storage share of COGS collapses**: previews are ~10% of COGS vs 93–97% for a storage-bearing plan [estimated: unit-economics.md §3]. The founder's "storage company" fear is avoided by construction, at the cost of not being the system of record (unit-economics.md Q6 ties backup-grade 91% retention to that position; this variant should expect the Photo & Video regime, not the Backblaze regime).
- **Payback.** Annual plan is cash-positive at purchase; economic payback at CAC $35 = 13 months of contribution [estimated: 35 ÷ 2.62]; LTV of contribution on a 15.6-month life ≈ $41 [estimated]; on a monthly plan with 1.9-month life ≈ $7.7 → monthly must be a minority of sales [estimated: unit-economics.md Q7 method].
- **What breaks at 10k families.** Compute does not (on-device). Support does: the merge-suggestion UI and consent flow are the tickets. Legal does if templates become company-readable (see Platform risk). Apple fees do above $1M proceeds (standard rate applies) [sourced: developer.apple.com SBP via assumptions/A5.md].

## Platform risk (the obvious feature Apple/Google/incumbent could ship; why this survives or doesn't)

- **The obvious slide: "People across your Shared Library" at WWDC 2027, or Google raising partner sharing above one partner with shared face labels.** Today neither exists (Apple: Shared Library article 2026-09-17 silent on People; MacRumors iOS 27: no changes to Shared Library/People; Google: labels private per account; 20 Google Photos blog posts Oct 2025→Oct 2026, 0 on sharing/merge) [sourced: assumptions/A4.md For #1–#3]. Probability by 2028-11 [unknown — no roadmap signal either way; A4].
- **If Apple ships it**, the all-Apple family (the majority: iOS 52% US traffic share; mixed households estimated 30–50% of multi-person) [estimated: sizing.md] loses most of the reason to pay. What survives:
  - the cross-platform case — Apple cannot index a Google-only relative's phone and Google cannot read iCloud; neither has shipped account merge [sourced: A4.md clause (a)];
  - the user-confirmed identity graph and annotations already built;
  - the BYO-AI endpoint and the XMP export.
  - Honest sizing of the survivor: 28–47M US mixed households × share who care [unknown].
- **If Google ships it**, Android/Google families are served inside Google; the Apple side of a mixed family is not.
- **Amazon Family Vault** already pools six members' uploads with natural-language search for Prime households [sourced: sizing.md Q6; aboutamazon.com explainer]; it requires uploading, is Prime-gated, and does not read iCloud/Google libraries. It is the cheapest "good enough" for Prime families who will upload; this variant's answer is "nothing to upload".
- **Apple could also close the door directly**: App Review 5.1.1(iii) (prefer the picker to full library access) and 5.1.2(vi) (no "use-based data mining" of Photo API data) [sourced: Apple guidelines via assumptions/A2.md Against #5]; a change restricting `BGContinuedProcessingTask` or ANE background access; or Siri AI gaining a user-selectable third-party model over Photos (A4 flip condition). Rejection likelihood [unknown].
- **Google Photos search regressions cut both ways**: users punished Ask Photos for being slower/worse than keyword search [sourced: androidpolice 2026-05-31 via byo-ai.md Q6]; the bar is "as fast as classic search", which on-device CLIP meets.
- **Net:** this survives Apple-only or Google-only moves because it is the only party that can sit on both sides; it does not survive both platforms shipping cross-account identity *and* the mixed-household share proving small. Rubric honesty: 5/10, not higher.

## Compounding asset

- **The confirmed identity graph.** Every confirmed merge ("Person 7 on Dad's phone = Mom's 'Grandma Ruth'") is a human decision that neither platform holds and neither exports (Apple: no People API on iOS; Google: labels private; neither platform's transfer tool carries faces) [sourced: platform-access.md Q1, Q5]. It grows with each member and each year and is re-derivable only by redoing the confirmations.
- **Annotations and provenance**: notes, dates on undated scans, "this is the lake house", which-library-holds-the-original — stored once against a cross-library asset map (dedupe decisions included).
- **Consent graph**: who has consented to be named; this is both a legal asset and a switching cost (face-regulation.md Q7).
- **Honest limits.** The index itself is rebuildable in minutes on-device for $0; MCP is a commodity (54,763 active registry servers; 22 "apple photos mcp" repos) [sourced: byo-ai.md Q5]. The compounding part is the human-confirmed layer, and the product's exit promise deliberately caps switching cost: names export as XMP `PersonInImage` + MWG regions readable by digiKam/Lightroom/exiftool; Apple Photos imports them only as keywords and Google documents no import — "portable names, not portable People albums" [sourced: ios-feasibility.md Q9]. Self-hosters already get the cross-user half free (Immich 3.3.0).

## Buildability (4-week demo spec for 2 engineers; ugliest technical risk)

**Demo (iOS 27 + one Mac; two engineers).**
- Week 1: PhotoKit enumeration; `PHCachingImageManager` thumbnail pass with network access off; MobileCLIP-S2 Core ML image/text towers (Queryable is the existence proof: on-device MobileCLIP search over the library, ~3.0k stars, iOS 18+) [sourced: github.com/mazzzystar/Queryable]; local HNSW/flat index; search UI.
- Week 2: faces — Vision face rectangles + a Core ML port of AdaFace (MIT) for embeddings (InsightFace buffalo_l is non-commercial) [sourced: ai-why-now.md Q3]; on-device HDBSCAN clusters; `BGContinuedProcessingTask` wrapper with Live Activity, chunked and resumable.
- Week 3: family space — per-member encrypted bundle (vectors, face vectors, 256 px previews, metadata) to R2; second device joins with the family key; merged search; cross-member match suggestions computed on the keeper's device; confirm/reject UI; XMP sidecar export.
- Week 4: assistant — Foundation Models on-device session with a `search_index` `Tool`; Claude/Gemini via the same `LanguageModelSession` behind a flag [sourced: ios-feasibility.md Q1]; a remote MCP server over the family index with OAuth and a Claude install link; a macOS 27 build sharing the index.
- Acceptance: a real 30k-asset iCloud-optimised library indexed in one session (target ≤30 min) [estimated: ios-feasibility.md Q3]; a second real relative's phone joined; "Grandma at the lake" returns hits from both libraries; one confirmed cross-account identity; export opens in digiKam with regions.

**Ugliest technical risk.** On-device face embedding + cross-device matching under the legal constraint. Apple publishes no face-embedding API (its <4 ms figure is internal) [sourced: machinelearning.apple.com 2021 via ios-feasibility.md Q3]; AdaFace-on-ANE latency is [unknown]; and the moment face vectors are exchanged between devices the Samsung "it never left the device" defence weakens — the Apple BIPA case's surviving subclass is precisely the *iCloud* one [sourced: face-regulation.md Q1]. The design answer (vectors leave only as ciphertext under a family key; matching on a member's device; naming gated on subject consent) has no case law behind it [unknown]. Second-ugliest: `BGContinuedProcessingTask` has no published max runtime and the iOS 27 ANE background entitlement's request path is undocumented (one developer report says it is absent from the 27.2 SDK) [sourced: ios-feasibility.md Q2]; a 30k index that is killed at minute 8 must resume cleanly. Third: the share of assets with no local copy under Optimize Storage [unknown] sets how much of the library the first session can cover. Kill criterion: if a 30k iCloud-optimised library cannot reach ≥80% coverage in ≤2 user-initiated sessions on an iPhone 15, the wedge fails.

## Ceiling (path to $100M ARR with tagged inputs and arithmetic; or the honest number it tops out at)

- Required payers: $100M ÷ $39.99/yr = **2.5M paying families**; at a $50 blended ARPU (some monthly), 2.0M [estimated].
- US pools: cross-platform households 28–47M; cross-platform and already paying for photo cloud 15–30M; all ≥2-account households ~78M [estimated: sizing.md]. 2.5M families = 8–17% of cross-platform payers, or 3.2% of all ≥2-account households [estimated].
- Comparables for a consumer family-photo subscription without a storage or newborn hook: Tinybeans ~95k paid at $75 ARPU; Ente 21,807 paid; Backblaze 402k consumer customers after ~18 years; the only nine-figure family app (FamilyAlbum, ¥17.1B segment, 12M MAU) rides a newborn wedge and a subsidy [sourced: customers.md Q1(h); unit-economics.md Q6; sizing.md chain (a)].
- Honest number: a well-run version reaching 3–5× Tinybeans' paid base (300k–500k families) at $40–50/yr is **$12–25M ARR** [estimated]. $100M requires international scale (global paid photo-cloud subscriptions ~300–400M, low confidence [estimated: sizing.md row 18]) plus a second revenue line — a pro/organizer seat or the storage system-of-record this variant explicitly refuses in v1. The path exists on paper; no comparable has walked it without a subsidy.

## Load-bearing assumptions (numbered; each with the observable fact that would falsify it)

1. **A one-session, user-initiated on-device index of a 30k iCloud-optimised library completes on iOS 27.** Falsified if: a measured run on an iPhone 15/16 covers <80% of assets in ≤2 sessions, or Apple documents a `BGContinuedProcessingTask` runtime cap below ~20 minutes.
2. **Cross-account identity + cross-library search is worth a second bill (~$40/yr) to enough mixed families.** Falsified if: a pre-sale/landing test converts <1% of qualified cross-platform families, or <30 of ~100 interviewed memory keepers pay a deposit (A3.md flip condition).
3. **The second relative installs.** Falsified if: invite→install <20% or median time-to-second-member >30 days in the first 500 families.
4. **Apple and Google do not ship cross-account People/identity sharing by 2028-11.** Falsified by: a People-sharing clause on support.apple.com/HT213248 or an Apple newsroom "family library" post; partner sharing above one person or shareable face labels on support.google.com/photos/answer/7378858 or /6128838 (A4 flip conditions).
5. **Family-keyed encrypted exchange of face vectors with on-device matching keeps the company outside BIPA/CUBI/GDPR Art. 9 "possession".** Falsified by: an Illinois state court rejecting G.T. v. Samsung reasoning, the Apple iCloud subclass prevailing on an encryption theory at trial (Law360 2026-10-09 reports the judge rejected Apple's encryption argument at summary judgment), or an EU DPA decision against a consumer app's face clustering.
6. **App Review accepts full-library access plus an on-device index as "core functionality".** Falsified by: rejection under 5.1.1(iii)/5.1.2(vi), or a policy restricting the upload/background APIs to vetted partners.
7. **Mixed-platform households are 30–50% of multi-person households and care.** Falsified by: a nat-rep survey putting mixed households <15%, or in-product data showing >85% of paying families are single-platform (which would make assumption 4 lethal).
8. **Retention lands in the annual Photo & Video regime (≥23% first renewal) rather than the monthly one.** Falsified by: first annual renewal <20% in cohort 1, or monthly plans >50% of sales.
9. **On-device models are good enough that users do not punish the search as Ask Photos was punished.** Falsified by: side-by-side recall on a family test set materially below Apple Photos search, or >20% of support tickets about "can't find" within 30 days.
10. **The free single-user tier does not become the whole user base.** Falsified by: paid-family conversion <5% of households with ≥2 installs after 90 days (RevenueCat trial→paid median is 22.2% for the category; a family tier should beat a trial).
