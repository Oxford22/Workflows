# v1-federation — Round 1 critique — TARGET CUSTOMER

Written 2026-11-03. Fresh context: I read SCORECARD.md, variants/v1-federation.md, and every file in research/ (including research/assumptions/). I did not read CHANGELOG, other variants, or other critiques.

## Stance

I am a non-technical adult in my 40s and the family "memory keeper." My parents are on iPhones (older ones), my brother is on Android, I pay for iCloud+ 200 GB ($2.99/mo) and I am not cancelling it. I have never opened a terminal. I field the "do you have the photo of Grandma at the lake?" texts. What I weigh: would I actually do the first step; what do I give up; who am I trusting; what happens if the startup dies; what this costs me versus what I do today (text my brother); and whether I would tell a friend to do it. Per the brief, Pain, Wedge and Distribution are scored hardest.

What I like, said once so it is on the record: nothing moves. My originals stay in iCloud, my parents' stay in theirs, and if the company folds I lose an index that was rebuilt on my phone for free and a set of names that come back as keywords. That is the right design for someone like me, and it is why this does not score as badly as v0 on Wedge. Everything below is why it still does not score well.

## Claims checked against the research

- **Failed:** "~15 min for 30,000 photos, ~22 min derated." research/ios-feasibility.md Q3 states the 15-minute figure is "a floor built from batch-1 model benchmarks, not a central estimate"; the central estimate is ~38 min and ~57 min derated. The variant derated the floor, not the central. For a non-technical user this is the difference between "wait while I make tea" and "leave the phone alone for an hour."
- **Failed (omission that changes the first 10 minutes):** the variant cites BGContinuedProcessingTask's "no published max runtime" but omits the DTS replies in ios-feasibility.md Q2 that expiry is final ("it wouldn't really make sense to 'resume' it later") and that opening the Camera expires the task ("There isn't anything you can do to prevent it"). My parents open the Camera.
- **Contradiction inside the variant:** the one-liner promises "iPhone, Mac, later Android" and the platform-risk section says the survivor if Apple ships People-sharing is the cross-platform household; the "Explicitly NOT in v1" list defers the Android index. For my household the cross-platform promise is not in the product I would be buying.
- **Supported:** iCloud+ 200 GB $2.99 (unit-economics.md Q5); Google face labels private per account (competitive.md Q2); Immich v3.3.0 cross-user people sharing 2026-10-07 (A3.md Against #3); Mylio Family $480–$732/yr (A3.md); Qeepsake CAC $35 (customers.md Q1(h)); index-only storage ≈ $0.02/mo (unit-economics.md Q7); Apple-Intelligence-capable 69.6% of TelemetryDeck top-10 (ios-feasibility.md Q5); PCC free under 2M downloads (ios-feasibility.md Q1 — note it also requires Small Business Program enrollment, which the variant does not mention).
- **Not verifiable from research:** "the CLIP/face index itself runs on any iPhone on iOS 18+ (Queryable)." ai-why-now.md and ios-feasibility.md cite Queryable as an existence proof but record no OS floor; the variant's claim rests on its own WebFetch. BGContinuedProcessingTask is iOS 26+, so whatever Queryable supports, the variant's "first 10 minutes" as written needs iOS 26/27 (ios-feasibility.md Q2), which is 36% (TelemetryDeck) to 16% (StatCounter) of the base for iOS 27 specifically (ios-feasibility.md Q5).

## The single most damaging objection

**"My brother is on Android, and your v1 says 'Android index yes, later.' Until that ships, what does my family get for $39.99 a year that iCloud Shared Photo Library — which my iCloud+ plan already includes for me plus five — does not?"**

Why this ends the conversation for me specifically: the variant's own platform-risk section says the all-Apple family "loses most of the reason to pay" if Apple ships People-sharing, and names the mixed household as the survivor. v1 cannot enroll the one person who makes my household mixed. What is left is an Apple-only family search, sold as a second bill, against Apple's free creator-plus-five Shared Library (competitive.md Q1; A4.md For #1) that my parents already understand the name of. The variant's differentiators on the Apple side — nothing moves, cross-member People suggestions, deep links back to each person's library — are real but are exactly the features Apple could add to Shared Library in one release, and the research found no evidence anyone pays for them today (A3.md (iii); trades-and-reddit.md Part B). If the founder cannot give an Android date and a reason the Apple-only version is worth a second bill in the meantime, I stay with texting.

## Weakest dimension

**Pain (3).** The research shows hours spent on export/merge and money spent on scanning, organizers and baby apps — none of which this variant sells. For the thing it does sell (search across relatives' phones without moving anything), I found no one paying, no one told to pay, and a free substitute (a text message, or Partner Sharing / Shared Library on one platform) that the research shows users like (customers.md Q1(c) HN comments). Without a demonstrated pain, the invite chain in Distribution never starts and the Ceiling math never applies.

## Three questions for the founder

1. When does the Android index ship, and until then what is the pitch to a family like mine (parents iPhone, brother Android) that is not "an Apple-only Shared Library with a second bill"?
2. If you shut down in 18 months, what exactly comes back to me, in what format, and will Apple Photos or Google Photos show the names my family confirmed as People — or only as keywords? (ios-feasibility.md Q9 says keywords on Apple, nothing documented on Google.)
3. My parents are in their 70s on iPhone 13s and will not sit through a Live Activity they might cancel by opening the Camera.
